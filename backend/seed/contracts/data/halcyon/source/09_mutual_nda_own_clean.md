MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of February 2, 2026, by and between Halcyon Care, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Halcyon"), and Juniper Analytics Corp, a healthcare analytics company organized under the laws of Delaware ("Counterparty"). Halcyon and Counterparty are each a "Disclosing Party" or "Receiving Party" as context requires, and the "Purpose" of this Agreement is to evaluate a potential commercial relationship involving Halcyon Engage.

RECITALS

A. Halcyon provides Halcyon Engage, a patient engagement and care coordination platform used by hospital systems and physician groups, and has developed proprietary technology, business processes and commercial information in connection with that platform.

B. Counterparty provides healthcare analytics services and has developed proprietary technology, methodologies and business information in connection with those services.

C. The parties wish to explore a potential commercial relationship involving Halcyon Engage, which may include integration between the parties' respective platforms, a referral or reseller arrangement, or another form of commercial collaboration to be agreed between the parties.

D. In connection with that exploration, each party may disclose to the other certain confidential and proprietary information, and the parties wish to protect the confidentiality of that information on the terms set out in this Agreement.

E. Nothing in this Agreement obligates either party to proceed with any commercial relationship, to disclose any particular information, or to continue discussions for any period of time, and either party may terminate discussions at any time for any reason.

F. The parties anticipate that discussions under this Agreement may include, among other topics, the technical architecture of each party's platform, the data elements each platform collects and generates, potential integration approaches, indicative commercial terms, and each party's roadmap for future product development, in each case only to the extent either party chooses to disclose that information.

G. Each party has independently determined that disclosing Confidential Information to the other party as contemplated by this Agreement, subject to the protections in this Agreement, is appropriate to allow the parties to evaluate the potential commercial relationship described in Recital C.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party"), whether orally, in writing or by inspection of premises, that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure, whether disclosed before or after the Effective Date. No marking or designation is required for information to be treated as Confidential Information.

1.2 Without limiting the generality of Section 1.1, Confidential Information includes each party's business plans, product roadmaps, pricing, financial information, customer and prospect lists, technical architecture, source code, and the terms of this Agreement and of any discussions between the parties concerning a potential commercial relationship.

1.3 "Affiliate" means, with respect to a party, any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that party, where "control" means the direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of an entity or the power to direct its management.

1.4 "Effective Date" means the date first written above.

1.5 "Representative" means, with respect to a party, that party's employees, officers, directors, Affiliates, and contractors and professional advisers bound by confidentiality obligations consistent with Section 2.2.

1.6 References in this Agreement to a party disclosing or receiving Confidential Information include disclosure or receipt by that party's Representatives acting on its behalf.

2. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

2.1 The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, but no less than reasonable care, and shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

2.2 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, officers, affiliates, contractors and professional advisers who have a need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by those it discloses to.

2.3 The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, disassemble or decompile any software or prototype disclosed as Confidential Information, except to the extent applicable law makes that restriction unenforceable.

2.4 Each party shall limit disclosure of the other party's Confidential Information within its own organization to those individuals described in Section 2.2, and shall not disclose Confidential Information more broadly than is reasonably necessary for the Purpose.

2.5 The Receiving Party shall promptly notify the Disclosing Party upon becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information, and shall reasonably cooperate with the Disclosing Party, at the Disclosing Party's expense, in any effort to remedy that unauthorized use or disclosure.

2.6 The obligations in this Section 2 apply equally to Confidential Information disclosed in tangible form, such as documents, drawings or storage media, and to Confidential Information disclosed orally or visually, provided that information disclosed orally or visually is treated as Confidential Information under Section 1.1 if a reasonable person would understand it to be confidential in the circumstances of disclosure.

3. EXCLUSIONS

3.1 Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was already known to the Receiving Party without an obligation of confidentiality before disclosure; (c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to the Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

3.2 The Receiving Party bears the burden of demonstrating that an exclusion listed in Section 3.1 applies to any information it uses or discloses in reliance on that exclusion, and a combination of individually excluded elements is not excluded merely because each element, taken alone, falls within Section 3.1.

4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

4.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, court order or a governmental or regulatory authority, provided it gives the Disclosing Party prompt prior notice where legally permitted and reasonable cooperation if the Disclosing Party seeks a protective order, and discloses only the minimum required.

4.2 If prior notice is not legally permitted, the Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party as soon as reasonably practicable after the disclosure is made, to the extent it is then permitted to do so.

5. RESIDUALS

5.1 This Agreement contains no residuals provision. Neither party may use or disclose ideas, concepts or know-how retained in the unaided memory of its personnel if doing so would require use or disclosure of the other party's Confidential Information.

6. EQUITABLE RELIEF

6.1 The parties acknowledge that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which damages alone would be an inadequate remedy, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedies available at law, without the necessity of posting a bond.

6.2 Nothing in this Section 6 limits either party's right to pursue any other remedy available to it at law or in equity for a breach or threatened breach of this Agreement.

7. NO LICENSE

7.1 Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting any licence, by estoppel or otherwise, in either party's intellectual property, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

7.2 As between the parties, each party retains all right, title and interest in and to its own Confidential Information, and any intellectual property embodied in it, subject only to the limited right described in Section 7.1.

8. TERM AND SURVIVAL

8.1 This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years from the Effective Date, during which period either party may disclose Confidential Information under its protection.

8.2 The confidentiality obligations in this Agreement survive for three (3) years after the end of the term, except that obligations relating to trade secrets survive for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

8.3 Expiration of the two-year period described in Section 8.1 does not itself terminate the obligations described in Section 8.2 with respect to Confidential Information disclosed during that period; it only ends the period during which the parties may disclose new Confidential Information under the protection of this Agreement.

8.4 "Trade secret" has the meaning given to that term under applicable law, and information does not cease to be a trade secret under Section 8.2 merely because it also falls within the definition of Confidential Information in Section 1.1.

9. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

9.1 Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession and certify in writing that it has done so, except for archival copies retained in routine backup systems or as required by applicable law or regulation.

9.2 Any Confidential Information retained under the exception in Section 9.1 remains subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement for as long as it is retained.

10. NON-SOLICITATION

10.1 During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other party with whom it had contact in connection with the Purpose, other than through general solicitations not targeted at that employee.

10.2 The restriction in Section 10.1 does not apply to an employee who responds to a general solicitation of employment not specifically directed at that employee or at employees of the other party generally, or to an employee whose employment with the other party ended before the solicitation began.

11. PUBLICITY

11.1 Neither party may use the other party's name, logo or trademarks without the other party's prior written consent.

11.2 Neither party may issue a press release or make a public statement about the discussions contemplated by this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except as required by applicable law or the rules of a stock exchange on which a party's securities are listed, in which case the disclosing party shall use reasonable efforts to give the other party advance notice and an opportunity to comment on the required disclosure.

12. GOVERNING LAW

12.1 This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

13. GENERAL PROVISIONS

13.1 This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

13.2 Notices shall be given in writing by email with confirmation of receipt or by nationally recognized courier to each party's address on the signature page.

13.3 This Agreement, together with any attachments identified on the signature page, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings, whether oral or written, concerning that subject matter. If the parties later enter into a definitive commercial agreement covering the same subject matter, the terms of that later agreement control to the extent of any conflict with this Agreement.

14. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

14.1 The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the parties, and neither party has authority to bind the other.

15. NO THIRD-PARTY BENEFICIARIES

15.1 This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their respective Affiliates described in Section 2.2, and does not create any right or remedy for any other person.

16. FURTHER ASSURANCES

16.1 Each party shall, at the other party's reasonable request, execute such further documents and take such further action as may be reasonably necessary to give full effect to the terms of this Agreement.

17. COUNTERPARTS AND ELECTRONIC SIGNATURE

17.1 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. A signature transmitted electronically, including by email of a scanned copy or by a recognized electronic-signature platform, is effective as an original signature.

18. INTERPRETATION

18.1 Headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. References to "Sections" are references to sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Unless the context requires otherwise, words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa, and "person" includes an individual, corporation, partnership and other entity.

19. SEVERABILITY

19.1 If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder of this Agreement remains in full force and effect, and the parties shall negotiate in good faith to replace the invalid or unenforceable provision with a valid provision that most closely reflects the original commercial intent.

20. WAIVER

20.1 No failure or delay by either party in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of that right, and no single or partial exercise of any right precludes any other or further exercise of that right. A waiver is effective only if made in writing and signed by the waiving party.

21. EXPORT CONTROL

21.1 Each party shall comply with applicable export control and economic sanctions laws in connection with its performance of this Agreement, and neither party shall export, re-export or transfer any technology or information provided under this Agreement in violation of those laws.

22. SURVIVAL OF TERMS

22.1 In addition to the confidentiality survival period described in Section 8.2, the following provisions survive any termination or expiration of this Agreement: Section 5 (Residuals), Section 6 (Equitable Relief), Section 7 (No License), Section 9 (Return or Destruction), Section 10 (Non-Solicitation) for the period stated in that Section, Section 11 (Publicity), Section 12 (Governing Law), this Section 22, and any other provision that by its nature is intended to survive.

22.2 Termination or expiration of this Agreement does not relieve either party of liability for a breach of this Agreement occurring before termination or expiration.

23. RELATIONSHIP TO FUTURE AGREEMENTS

23.1 If the parties enter into a separate written agreement that expressly addresses the confidentiality of information exchanged in connection with the subject matter of that agreement, this Agreement continues to apply to Confidential Information disclosed before the effective date of that separate agreement, unless that separate agreement expressly states otherwise.

SCHEDULE A: ILLUSTRATIVE DISCUSSION TOPICS

This Schedule is illustrative only and does not expand or limit the definition of Confidential Information in Section 1.1. The parties anticipate that discussions under this Agreement may touch on the following topics, among others, to the extent either party chooses to disclose information about them.

Halcyon topics: the architecture of the Halcyon Engage platform and its HL7 FHIR integration with hospital record systems; Halcyon's approach to multi-tenant data separation; aggregate, de-identified usage patterns across Halcyon's customer base that Halcyon may describe for illustrative purposes; and indicative commercial terms on which Halcyon might make Halcyon Engage data or functionality available to Counterparty.

Counterparty topics: Counterparty's healthcare analytics methodology and the categories of data it typically analyzes; Counterparty's approach to validating analytic models against clinical outcomes; and indicative commercial terms on which Counterparty might provide analytics services using data made available through an integration with Halcyon Engage.

Joint topics: potential integration architectures between the two platforms; potential allocation of responsibility for data protection and security obligations under any future commercial agreement; and a timeline for moving from this exploratory discussion to a definitive agreement, if the parties decide to proceed.

Nothing in this Schedule obligates either party to disclose information about any topic listed above, and a party's decision not to disclose information about a listed topic is not a breach of this Agreement.

SCHEDULE B: ADMINISTRATION OF THIS AGREEMENT

Each party shall designate a relationship lead responsible for coordinating discussions under this Agreement and for receiving notices under Section 13.2, and shall identify that individual on the signature page or by separate written notice. A party may change its designated relationship lead by written notice to the other party.

Each party shall maintain a record of the Confidential Information it discloses to the other party under this Agreement and the date of disclosure, to the extent reasonably practicable, so that it can identify what it disclosed if a dispute arises concerning the scope of Confidential Information covered by this Agreement. Neither party's failure to maintain such a record limits the protection this Agreement affords to information that otherwise meets the definition in Section 1.1.

The parties may, by mutual written agreement, extend the two-year period described in Section 8.1 for one or more additional periods, without the need to execute an entirely new agreement, provided the extension is recorded in writing and signed by both parties.

If either party engages a Representative described in Section 1.5 who is not an employee, that party remains responsible for that Representative's compliance with this Agreement to the same extent as if that Representative were its own employee, and shall take reasonable steps to ensure the Representative is aware of the obligations in this Agreement before receiving Confidential Information.

SCHEDULE C: BACKGROUND INFORMATION

The following background information is provided for context and does not create any obligation beyond those set out in the body of this Agreement.

Halcyon Care, Inc. is a Delaware corporation headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, and provides Halcyon Engage, a multi-tenant software-as-a-service platform used by hospital systems and physician groups across the United States to manage appointment reminders, secure messaging, intake forms and care-plan tracking. Halcyon Engage integrates with hospital record systems over HL7 FHIR. Halcyon maintains SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001 and HITRUST r2 certifications, and holds its own cyber and technology errors and omissions insurance, as described in the materials Halcyon makes available to prospective commercial counterparties in the ordinary course.

Juniper Analytics Corp is a Delaware corporation that provides healthcare analytics services, including the kind of analytic methodology referenced in Schedule A, to hospital systems and other participants in the healthcare industry. Juniper Analytics Corp and Halcyon have had preliminary conversations about a possible integration between Juniper Analytics Corp's analytics services and the Halcyon Engage platform, and this Agreement is intended to facilitate those conversations by giving each party confidence that information it shares will be protected.

Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to review this Agreement, has had the opportunity to consult with legal counsel of its choosing concerning this Agreement, and enters into this Agreement voluntarily and with a full understanding of its terms.
