MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of September 15, 2026, between Swiftline Cloud Services LLC, a cloud messaging vendor organized under the laws of Nevada ("Vendor"), and Halcyon Care, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Halcyon"), under which Vendor will provide SMS and email delivery infrastructure used by Halcyon Engage. This Agreement is on Vendor's standard order-form terms of service.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Halcyon operates Halcyon Engage, a multi-tenant patient engagement and care coordination platform that sends appointment reminders, secure messaging notifications and other patient communications on behalf of approximately 310 hospital system and physician group customers;

WHEREAS, Vendor operates a cloud-based messaging delivery platform that provides SMS and email transmission infrastructure, including message routing, delivery confirmation and basic analytics, to business customers across a range of industries;

WHEREAS, Halcyon wishes to engage Vendor to provide SMS and email delivery infrastructure that Halcyon Engage will use to transmit patient communications, including appointment reminders and other notifications that reference de-identified patient contact information, and Vendor is willing to provide the Services on the terms set out in this Agreement and the applicable Order Form;

WHEREAS, the parties intend that this Agreement sets out the general terms that will govern one or more Order Forms executed from time to time, each of which incorporates this Agreement by reference and specifies the particular Services, Fees and other commercial terms applicable to that Order Form;

WHEREAS, Halcyon's selection of a messaging infrastructure vendor follows an internal review process conducted by Halcyon's contracts and security teams, covering the vendor's delivery reliability, data handling practices, and ability to meet Halcyon's buy-side contracting standards for vendors that process data originating from Halcyon's own customers, even where that data does not itself constitute protected health information;

WHEREAS, Vendor has represented to Halcyon that the Services are suitable for high-volume transactional messaging of the kind Halcyon Engage generates, including appointment reminder and notification traffic that may need to be delivered reliably and promptly to be useful to Halcyon's customers and their patients;

WHEREAS, the parties anticipate that Halcyon's use of the Services may grow over the term of this Agreement as additional Halcyon customers are onboarded to Halcyon Engage, and the parties have structured this Agreement, together with the Order Form mechanism described in Section 1.2, to accommodate that anticipated growth without requiring a new master agreement for each additional customer onboarded;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth below, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Services" means Vendor's messaging delivery platform. "Halcyon Data" means data Halcyon submits to the Services, including de-identified patient contact information. "Fees" means the amounts payable under the Order Form.

1.2 "Order Form" means a written or electronic order document, executed or accepted by both parties, that references this Agreement and specifies the Services ordered, the Fees, the Initial Term, and any Service-specific terms. Each Order Form forms part of this Agreement upon execution.

1.3 "Documentation" means Vendor's then-current online user guides, API reference materials and other documentation made generally available to Vendor's customers describing the features and operation of the Services.

1.4 "Authorized Users" means Halcyon's employees and contractors who are authorized by Halcyon to access and use the Services on Halcyon's behalf, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

1.5 "Affiliate" means, with respect to a party, any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of an entity.

1.6 "Confidential Information" has the meaning given in Section 11.

2. SERVICES

2.1 Vendor warrants that the Services will perform substantially as described in Vendor's online documentation during the term of this Agreement.

2.2 Vendor shall make the Services available to Halcyon and its Authorized Users for Halcyon's internal business purposes, including the transmission of SMS and email communications generated by Halcyon Engage to patients and other recipients designated by Halcyon's customers, in accordance with this Agreement, the Documentation and the applicable Order Form.

2.3 Vendor shall provide the Services in a manner consistent with applicable telecommunications and messaging regulations, including applicable rules governing unsolicited messages, and shall maintain the technical capability to process opt-out and unsubscribe requests received through the Services in accordance with the Documentation.

2.4 Halcyon is responsible for the content of the messages it submits to the Services, for obtaining any consents required from message recipients under applicable law, and for configuring the Services in accordance with the Documentation. Vendor is responsible for the technical transmission of messages submitted through the Services in accordance with this Agreement.

2.5 From time to time, the parties may execute a Statement of Work describing professional services, custom integration work, or implementation assistance that Vendor will provide in addition to the standard Services, on the terms set out in Section 17.

2.6 Vendor shall provide Halcyon with reasonable access to technical support during Vendor's normal business hours for questions concerning the use of the Services, and shall make available to Halcyon the Documentation necessary for Halcyon's Authorized Users to configure and operate the Services as contemplated by this Agreement.

2.7 Vendor may update the Services from time to time, including by releasing new features or modifying existing functionality, provided that Vendor shall not materially reduce the core functionality of the Services that Halcyon has ordered without providing Halcyon with reasonable advance notice.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Halcyon shall pay the Fees within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

<!-- Net 30 payment term, shorter than Halcyon's standard buy-side ask of Net 60. -->

3.2 Vendor shall invoice Halcyon on a monthly basis for Fees incurred during the preceding month, unless the applicable Order Form specifies a different invoicing frequency. Each invoice shall include reasonable supporting detail sufficient for Halcyon to verify the charges, including the volume of messages sent during the invoiced period.

3.3 If Halcyon in good faith disputes a portion of an invoice, Halcyon shall pay the undisputed portion by the due date and shall notify Vendor of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute, and the parties shall work together in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

3.4 Fees are stated and payable in United States dollars unless the applicable Order Form states otherwise. Halcyon shall make payment by the method specified in the Order Form, which may include wire transfer or automated clearing house transfer to an account Vendor designates in writing.

4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 This Agreement has an initial term of twelve (12) months from the Effective Date, renewing automatically for successive twelve-month terms unless either party gives sixty (60) days' notice of non-renewal.

4.2 Either party may terminate for the other party's uncured material breach after fifteen (15) days' written notice.

4.3 Termination of this Agreement does not relieve Halcyon of its obligation to pay Fees accrued before the effective date of termination, and does not limit either party's right to pursue any remedy available to it for the other party's breach.

4.4 A notice of non-renewal or termination under this Section 4 shall be given in writing in accordance with Section 16.5, and is effective upon the other party's receipt unless this Section 4 states a specific notice period, in which case the notice period runs from the date of receipt.

5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 Vendor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available not less than 99.5% of the time each month.

5.2 Scheduled maintenance performed with at least twenty-four (24) hours' advance notice to Halcyon does not count against the uptime commitment in Section 5.1. Vendor shall use reasonable efforts to schedule maintenance outside of Halcyon's peak messaging hours where practicable.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 Each party shall protect the other's confidential information using reasonable care.

11.2 For purposes of this Section 11, "confidential information" means non-public information disclosed by one party to the other that is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure or that should reasonably be understood to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure, but excludes information that is public, already known to the receiving party, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party.

11.3 Each party may disclose the other party's confidential information to its own employees, contractors and professional advisers who have a need to know it to perform or receive the Services, provided such persons are bound by confidentiality obligations consistent with this Section 11, and each party remains responsible for their compliance.

11.4 The obligations of confidentiality in this Section 11 do not restrict Vendor's use of aggregated or de-identified data derived from Halcyon Data, provided such data does not identify Halcyon, any Halcyon customer, or any individual, and is used by Vendor solely to monitor, maintain and improve the operation of the Services generally across Vendor's customer base.

12. SECURITY

12.1 Vendor shall maintain a written information security program with safeguards appropriate to the data it processes.

12.2 Vendor's information security program shall address, at a minimum, access controls limiting access to Halcyon Data to personnel who need it to perform the Services, encryption of Halcyon Data in transit over public networks, and a process for applying security patches to systems that process Halcyon Data within a reasonable time after a patch becomes available. Vendor shall periodically review and update its information security program to address changes in technology and applicable threats.

12.3 Vendor shall not use Halcyon Data for any purpose other than providing the Services to Halcyon, including training any machine learning or artificial intelligence model that is not dedicated solely to providing the Services to Halcyon, unless Halcyon gives its prior written consent.

13. SECURITY INCIDENTS

13.1 Vendor shall use reasonable efforts to inform Halcyon of a security incident affecting Halcyon Data after Vendor becomes aware of one, following completion of Vendor's internal investigation into the incident. This Agreement does not state a specific time limit for that notice.

15.2 If Vendor does not maintain a current SOC 2 report, Vendor shall, upon Halcyon's request under Section 15.1, provide a written response to a reasonable security questionnaire covering the safeguards described in Section 12 instead of the report.

Where a Statement of Work and an Order Form both relate to the same engagement, the Order Form governs the ongoing Fees for the standard Services, and the Statement of Work governs the scope and fees for the supplemental services described in it, unless the documents state otherwise.
