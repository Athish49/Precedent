SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Software Subscription and Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of January 12, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), between Castleview Memorial Hospital System, a not-for-profit hospital system organized under the laws of Illinois ("Customer"), and Halcyon Care, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Halcyon"). Customer and Halcyon are each a "party" and together the "parties." This Agreement is on Customer's standard vendor paper, as issued by Customer's procurement and legal department.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Customer is a not-for-profit hospital system that provides patient care across multiple facilities in Illinois and wishes to license a patient engagement and care coordination platform for use by its clinical and administrative personnel;

WHEREAS, Halcyon has developed and operates Halcyon Engage, a multi-tenant software-as-a-service platform offering appointment reminders, secure messaging, intake forms and care-plan tracking, and integrating with hospital record systems over HL7 FHIR;

WHEREAS, Customer's procurement and legal department prepared this Agreement on Customer's standard vendor contracting paper, and the parties executed a Business Associate Agreement concurrently with this Agreement to address Halcyon's handling of protected health information as a business associate of Customer; and

WHEREAS, the parties wish to set out the terms on which Halcyon will provide the Services to Customer, with the subscription fees and Subscription Term set out in Exhibit A to this Agreement;

WHEREAS, Customer's clinical and administrative personnel will rely on the Services to coordinate care across Customer's facilities, and Customer's procurement and legal department has accordingly negotiated terms intended to protect Customer's patients and its regulatory position as a covered entity under HIPAA;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Services" means the Halcyon Engage patient engagement and care coordination platform licensed to Customer under this Agreement.

1.2 "Customer Data" means all data, including patient and clinical data, that Customer or its personnel submit to the Services.

1.3 "Documentation" means Halcyon's user manuals for the Services.

1.4 "Fees" means the subscription fees set out in Exhibit A.

1.5 "Subscription Term" has the meaning given in Section 4.

1.6 "Authorized Users" means Customer's employees, medical staff and contractors who are authorized by Customer to access and use the Services.

1.7 "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including the terms of this Agreement, Customer Data and Halcyon's non-public product and security information.

1.8 "Breach" has the meaning given to that term, or to the term "breach," in the HIPAA Rules, and includes any acquisition, access, use or disclosure of PHI not permitted by this Agreement or the Business Associate Agreement.

1.9 "Business Associate Agreement" means the business associate agreement entered into by the parties concurrently with this Agreement, governing Halcyon's creation, receipt, maintenance and transmission of PHI on Customer's behalf.

1.10 "PHI" means protected health information, as defined in the HIPAA Rules, that Halcyon creates, receives, maintains or transmits on Customer's behalf in connection with the Services.

1.11 "Subcontractor" means a person or entity to which Halcyon delegates a function, activity or service involving the creation, receipt, maintenance or transmission of PHI on Halcyon's behalf.

1.12 "Applicable Law" means the laws, regulations and binding regulatory guidance applicable to a party's performance of its obligations under this Agreement, including the HIPAA Rules.

1.13 "HIPAA Rules" means the Privacy, Security, Breach Notification and Enforcement Rules at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164, as amended.

1.14 "Minimum Necessary" means the minimum necessary standard described in the HIPAA Rules, limiting the use, disclosure and request of PHI to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose.

1.15 "Scheduled Maintenance" means planned downtime of the Services for upgrades, patches or infrastructure work for which Halcyon gives Customer advance notice in accordance with the Documentation.

1.16 "Affiliate" means, with respect to a party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that party.

1.17 "Malicious Code" means any code, file or program designed to disrupt, damage or gain unauthorized access to a computer system or network, including viruses, worms, time bombs and Trojan horses.

2. SERVICES AND WARRANTIES

2.1 Halcyon shall provide the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner materially consistent with the Documentation.

2.2 Halcyon shall make the Services available for use by Customer's Authorized Users, including clinical and administrative personnel across Customer's facilities, for the purposes described in the Documentation.

2.3 Halcyon shall provide Customer with reasonable implementation assistance, including configuration support for integrating the Services with Customer's hospital record systems over HL7 FHIR, as described in the Documentation.

2.4 Customer shall be responsible for the accuracy of the Customer Data it submits to the Services and for ensuring that its Authorized Users comply with this Agreement and with Customer's own policies governing access to patient information.

2.5 Customer shall not, and shall ensure that its Authorized Users do not: (a) reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Services; (b) use the Services to transmit Malicious Code; or (c) use the Services in a way that interferes with Halcyon's provision of the Services to other customers.

2.6 Each party represents and warrants to the other that it is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of its jurisdiction of organization, and that it has full corporate power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Customer shall pay the Fees within forty-five (45) days of receipt of a correct invoice.

3.2 Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes, which Customer shall bear except for taxes on Halcyon's net income.

4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 This Agreement has an initial term of thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date, renewing automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before renewal.

4.2 Either party may terminate for the other party's uncured material breach after thirty (30) days' written notice.

4.3 Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, Halcyon shall, at Customer's election, return or destroy all Customer Data in Halcyon's possession, except to the extent Halcyon is required by Applicable Law to retain copies, in which case Halcyon shall continue to protect that data in accordance with this Agreement and the Business Associate Agreement for as long as it is retained.

4.4 Customer's right to access the Services ceases upon the effective date of termination or expiration, and all Fees accrued but unpaid as of that date become immediately due and payable.

4.5 For ninety (90) days following notice of non-renewal or termination, Halcyon shall reasonably cooperate with Customer's transition to a successor platform, including by making Customer Data available for export in a commonly used electronic format.

5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 Halcyon shall make the Services available not less than 99.9% of the time each month, excluding scheduled maintenance, with service credits available on request.

5.2 Halcyon shall give Customer reasonable advance notice of scheduled maintenance that is expected to affect the availability of the Services, and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to schedule such maintenance outside Customer's normal business hours where practicable.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

6.1 EACH PARTY'S LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT, HOWEVER CAUSED AND ON ANY THEORY OF LIABILITY, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT OR OTHERWISE, SHALL BE UNLIMITED. THE PARTIES ACKNOWLEDGE THAT PATIENT DATA HANDLED UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IS OF A SENSITIVITY AND VALUE THAT CANNOT BE REDUCED TO A FIXED MONETARY LIMIT, AND ACCORDINGLY NO CAP ON EITHER PARTY'S LIABILITY SHALL APPLY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT OR THE ASSOCIATED BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT.

6.2 Nothing in this Agreement shall exclude either party's liability for indirect, consequential or punitive damages; each party shall be liable for the full extent of all loss, of whatever kind, arising from a breach of this Agreement.

7. INDEMNIFICATION

7.1 Halcyon shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer against any third-party claim, including a regulatory claim, arising from Halcyon's provision of the Services, and shall further indemnify Customer against any loss arising from Customer's own use of the Services, regardless of cause.

7.2 Customer shall give Halcyon prompt written notice of any claim for which Customer seeks indemnification under this Section 7, and shall reasonably cooperate, at Halcyon's expense, in the defense of that claim. Customer may participate in the defense with counsel of its own choosing, at its own expense.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION

8.1 Halcyon shall use Customer Data solely to provide the Services and shall not use Customer Data for any secondary purpose, including benchmarking or analytics across other Halcyon customers, without Customer's separate prior written consent for each use.

8.2 Halcyon shall notify Customer of any Breach involving PHI within twenty-four (24) hours after Halcyon first becomes aware, whether confirmed or suspected, of circumstances indicating a possible Breach.

8.3 Halcyon's notification under Section 8.2 shall describe, to the extent then known, the nature of the Breach, the categories and approximate number of individuals affected, and the steps Halcyon is taking to investigate and mitigate the Breach, with updates provided as more information becomes available.

8.4 Halcyon shall reasonably cooperate with Customer's investigation of any Breach, including by making relevant logs and personnel available to Customer upon request.

8.5 Halcyon shall mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect of a Breach that is known to Halcyon, and shall document the Breach, its investigation, and the resulting mitigation in accordance with the HIPAA Rules.

9. SECURITY

9.1 Halcyon shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards for electronic PHI consistent with the HIPAA Security Rule at 45 C.F.R. Part 164, Subpart C, and shall promptly remediate any gap identified by Customer.

9.2 Halcyon's safeguards include, without limitation, encryption of electronic PHI at rest and in transit, role-based access controls, workforce security training, and maintenance of SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001 and HITRUST r2 certifications covering the Services.

9.3 Halcyon shall promptly notify Customer of any material change to its security program that reduces the safeguards described in this Section 9.

9.4 Halcyon hosts the Services, and stores Customer Data, on infrastructure located in AWS `us-east-1` and `us-west-2`, consistent with Halcyon's standard hosting regions for its United States customers.

10. SUBCONTRACTORS

10.1 Halcyon represents that it has entered into business associate agreements with each of its Subcontractors that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on Halcyon's behalf, containing restrictions on the use and disclosure of PHI that are no less protective than those in this Agreement; Customer may request a summary of those flow-down terms from Halcyon's privacy office.

10.2 Halcyon shall maintain a current list of its Subcontractors that create, receive, maintain or transmit PHI on Halcyon's behalf, and shall make that list available to Customer upon written request.

10.3 Halcyon shall notify Customer in writing before engaging a new Subcontractor that will create, receive, maintain or transmit PHI on Halcyon's behalf, and shall ensure that any such Subcontractor is bound by a business associate agreement consistent with Section 10.1 before being given access to PHI.

11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

11.1 Halcyon retains all right, title and interest in and to the Services, the Documentation and all underlying technology. No rights are granted to Customer except as expressly set out in this Agreement.

11.2 Halcyon may use any suggestions, enhancement requests or feedback Customer provides about the Services under a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence, without obligation to Customer.

12. AUDIT

12.1 Customer, acting directly or through a state or federal regulator, auditor or inspector general, may audit and inspect Halcyon's facilities, systems, personnel and records relevant to this Agreement, including by on-site inspection, at any time and as often as Customer considers necessary, without advance notice, to verify Halcyon's compliance with this Agreement and with HIPAA. Halcyon shall provide Customer's auditors with direct access to the production environment hosting Customer Data on request.
