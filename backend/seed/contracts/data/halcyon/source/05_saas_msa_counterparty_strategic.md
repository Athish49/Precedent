ENTERPRISE SOFTWARE AGREEMENT

This Enterprise Software Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of May 1, 2026, between Pinnacle National Health Alliance, a multi-state hospital network organized under the laws of New York ("Customer"), and Halcyon Care, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Halcyon"). This Agreement is on Customer's standard enterprise procurement paper. The parties acknowledge that the Fees under the attached Order Form exceed $1,800,000 per year, making this a strategic transaction for Halcyon.

Customer operates hospitals and affiliated physician groups across multiple states and selected Halcyon Engage, following a competitive procurement process, to standardize patient engagement and care coordination across its network. Given the scale of the engagement, the parties have negotiated certain commercial and risk terms that depart from Halcyon's standard terms and that Halcyon has agreed to offer in recognition of the deal's size, the multi-year commitment described in the Order Form, and Customer's status as one of Halcyon's largest customers by annual Fees. The parties intend this Agreement, together with the Order Form, to be the complete and exclusive statement of their agreement concerning the Services. Each party represents that it has the full corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and that the individual signing on its behalf is duly authorized to do so.

Customer's procurement process involved evaluation of Halcyon's platform by Customer's clinical informatics, information security and procurement teams across several of Customer's hospital sites, and the commercial terms in this Agreement reflect the outcome of that process together with Customer's standard enterprise procurement requirements. Halcyon's account team for this engagement will include a dedicated customer success manager and a named technical point of contact for the implementation described in Exhibit A.

This Order Form is entered into under, and is governed by, the Enterprise Software Agreement between Pinnacle National Health Alliance and Halcyon Care, Inc. dated May 1, 2026.

EXHIBIT B - SUPPORT AND MAINTENANCE

Incident response: Halcyon will acknowledge support tickets submitted by Customer's designated administrators within one (1) business day, and will prioritize incidents according to their severity, with incidents that render the Services wholly unavailable across Customer's network receiving priority attention on a twenty-four hour basis consistent with the premium support tier purchased under the Order Form.

Maintenance windows: Halcyon performs routine maintenance outside of peak usage hours where practicable and will give Customer at least twenty-four (24) hours' advance notice of any maintenance window reasonably expected to affect the availability of the Services.

Status reporting: Halcyon maintains a status page reporting the operational status of the Services and will post updates during any incident affecting availability.

Software updates: Halcyon may release updates, patches and new versions of the Services from time to time as part of the Subscription Term, at no additional charge, subject to Section 2.3.

Account governance: Halcyon and Customer will hold quarterly business reviews during the Subscription Term to review service performance, the implementation roadmap, and any planned changes to the Services, reflecting the scale of this engagement.

Escalation: if Customer is not satisfied with Halcyon's handling of a support ticket, Customer's designated administrator may escalate the matter to Halcyon's customer success manager for this account, and, if still unresolved, to Halcyon's VP of Customer Success, who will coordinate a resolution plan with Customer's procurement and clinical informatics contacts within five (5) business days of the escalation.

EXHIBIT C - GOVERNANCE AND REPORTING

Halcyon will provide Customer with a quarterly operational report summarizing the Monthly Uptime Percentage achieved for each month in the quarter, any service credits issued under Section 5.2, a summary of support tickets by severity, and the status of any open items from the most recent quarterly business review. The parties will use the quarterly business review described in Exhibit B to discuss this report, Customer's planned expansion of Authorized Users across additional hospitals or physician groups within its network, and any updates to the implementation roadmap described in Exhibit A.

The quarterly operational report will be delivered to Customer's designated procurement and clinical informatics contacts no later than fifteen (15) business days after the end of each calendar quarter, and will be treated as Confidential Information of both parties under Section 7.
