BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT

This Business Associate Agreement (this "BAA") is entered into as of July 7, 2026, between Brightwater County Hospital, a county hospital organized under the laws of Texas ("Covered Entity"), and Halcyon Care, Inc. ("Business Associate"), and supplements the Master Subscription Agreement between the parties (the "Underlying Agreement"). This BAA is drafted on Covered Entity's standard county-procurement BAA template. Several terms that Halcyon's own template would ordinarily include have been omitted from Covered Entity's template, as reflected in the Gap Notes following each section.

RECITALS

A. Covered Entity is a county hospital organized under the laws of Texas and is a "covered entity" as that term is defined at 45 C.F.R. § 160.103.

B. Covered Entity has engaged Business Associate under the Underlying Agreement to provide the Halcyon Engage patient engagement and care coordination platform, and in the course of providing that platform Business Associate creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on behalf of Covered Entity.

C. Covered Entity's procurement department requires that any vendor handling PHI on its behalf sign a business associate agreement on Covered Entity's own standard county-procurement form, and this BAA is that form as presented to Business Associate.

D. The parties intend this BAA to satisfy the requirement under 45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e) that a covered entity obtain satisfactory assurances, in the form of a written contract, that a business associate will appropriately safeguard PHI, to the extent the terms of this BAA, as presented, address that requirement.

E. This BAA supplements, and is incorporated by reference into, the Underlying Agreement, and the parties acknowledge that the Underlying Agreement contains general information-security and confidentiality terms that apply to Business Associate's handling of Covered Entity's information generally, in addition to the specific terms of this BAA.

F. Business Associate provides the Halcyon Engage platform to approximately 310 customers nationally, including hospital systems, physician groups and county and municipal hospital districts, and maintains a single set of internal policies and procedures applicable across its customer base, subject to the specific commitments it makes in a given customer's business associate agreement.

G. Covered Entity operates as a county hospital district and is one of a number of county and municipal hospital districts in Texas that use county-procurement forms, rather than a vendor's standard contract forms, for agreements involving the handling of PHI by a technology vendor.

H. Covered Entity's procurement department selected this BAA form, rather than a form proposed by Business Associate, as part of its standard process for engaging technology vendors, and Business Associate has executed this BAA as presented in order to proceed with the Underlying Agreement.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Business Associate" means Halcyon Care, Inc. "Covered Entity" means Brightwater County Hospital. "PHI" means protected health information as defined at 45 C.F.R. § 160.103 that Business Associate creates, receives, maintains or transmits on behalf of Covered Entity. "Subcontractor" means a person to whom Business Associate delegates a function involving PHI. "Breach" has the meaning given at 45 C.F.R. § 164.402.

1.2 "HIPAA Rules" means the Privacy, Security, Breach Notification and Enforcement Rules at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164. "Required by Law" has the meaning given at 45 C.F.R. § 164.103. "Secretary" means the Secretary of the United States Department of Health and Human Services or the Secretary's designee. "Unsecured PHI" means PHI that is not rendered unusable, unreadable or indecipherable to unauthorized persons through a technology or methodology specified by the Secretary.

1.3 Capitalized terms used in this BAA that are not otherwise defined in this BAA have the meanings given to them in the Underlying Agreement or, where the Underlying Agreement does not define a term used in this BAA, the meanings given to those terms in the HIPAA Rules.

1.4 "Electronic PHI" means PHI that is transmitted by or maintained in electronic media, as those terms are used in the HIPAA Rules. "Designated Record Set" has the meaning given at 45 C.F.R. § 164.501. "Services" means the services Business Associate provides to Covered Entity under the Underlying Agreement.

1.5 References in this BAA to "day" mean calendar day unless otherwise stated.

2. PERMITTED USES AND DISCLOSURES

2.1 Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the Services described in the Underlying Agreement, and as otherwise permitted or required by this BAA or by law.

2.2 Business Associate shall not use or disclose PHI in any manner that would violate the HIPAA Rules if done by Covered Entity itself, except as this BAA otherwise permits. Business Associate shall not sell PHI, and shall not use PHI to market or advertise a third party's products or services, in each case except to the extent affirmatively permitted by Covered Entity in writing.

SCHEDULE 1: DESCRIPTION OF SERVICES

This Schedule describes, at a summary level, the Services under the Underlying Agreement in connection with which Business Associate creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI. This Schedule is descriptive only and does not expand or limit the scope of Section 2.

Business Associate provides Covered Entity with the Halcyon Engage platform, which supports appointment reminders, secure messaging between Covered Entity's clinical staff and patients, electronic intake forms completed by patients before a visit, and care-plan tracking for patients enrolled in a care program by Covered Entity's clinical staff. The Halcyon Engage platform integrates with Covered Entity's hospital record system over HL7 FHIR to retrieve and display the information necessary to perform these functions.

In the course of providing the Halcyon Engage platform, Business Associate creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI including patient names, contact details, appointment information, intake-form responses and secure-messaging content exchanged between Covered Entity's clinical staff and patients. Business Associate does not receive laboratory results, diagnostic images or clinical notes through the Halcyon Engage platform; those records remain in Covered Entity's hospital record system and are not transmitted to Business Associate.

Covered Entity's clinical staff access the Halcyon Engage platform through Covered Entity's existing single sign-on arrangement under the Underlying Agreement, and Covered Entity is responsible for provisioning and deprovisioning its own staff accounts in accordance with the Underlying Agreement. Patients access the appointment reminder, secure messaging and intake-form functions through a patient-facing application that Business Associate makes available as part of the Halcyon Engage platform.

Covered Entity's clinical departments that make use of the Halcyon Engage platform as of the effective date of this BAA include its outpatient scheduling department, its care coordination department, and the physician practices affiliated with Covered Entity that participate in its shared scheduling system. Additional departments may begin using the Halcyon Engage platform over the term of the Underlying Agreement without requiring an amendment to this Schedule, provided the categories of PHI involved remain consistent with those described above.

The description in this Schedule reflects the Services as of the effective date of this BAA. If Covered Entity and Business Associate later agree to add a Service under the Underlying Agreement that involves a materially different category of PHI than described above, the parties shall consider whether this Schedule, or the balance of this BAA, needs to be updated to reflect that addition, consistent with the amendment process under Section 11.1.
