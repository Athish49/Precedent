DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of June 9, 2026, between Vantage Point Diagnostics, Inc., a diagnostic laboratory network organized under the laws of New Jersey ("Controller"), and Halcyon Care, Inc. ("Processor"), and supplements the underlying services agreement between the parties (the "Services Agreement"). This Agreement is drafted on Controller's standard vendor data-protection template.

RECITALS

A. Controller operates a network of diagnostic laboratories and ordering physician practices across the mid-Atlantic region and has engaged Processor to provide the Halcyon Engage patient engagement and care coordination platform under the Services Agreement.

B. In the course of providing the services described in the Services Agreement, Processor will process personal data that Controller determines the purposes and means of processing, and the parties intend this Agreement to set out the terms on which that processing takes place.

C. Controller has reviewed Processor's standard data-protection terms and has instead presented this Agreement on its own vendor template, reflecting Controller's standard approach to engaging technology vendors that process personal data of patients and referring physicians.

D. The parties wish to record their respective roles, the scope and purpose of the processing, and the safeguards that will apply, and this Agreement forms part of the contractual arrangements between Controller and Processor and is incorporated by reference into the Services Agreement.

E. Controller's laboratory and physician-practice network is subject to a range of state and federal privacy obligations arising from its handling of patient information, and Controller has adopted an internal policy requiring that any vendor processing personal data on Controller's behalf do so under a written agreement addressing the matters set out in this Agreement.

F. Processor has represented to Controller that it maintains SOC 2 Type II and ISO/IEC 27001 certifications covering the Halcyon Engage platform, and Controller has relied on those representations, together with the terms of this Agreement, in agreeing to engage Processor to process personal data as described in this Agreement.

G. The parties acknowledge that this Agreement may need to be updated from time to time to reflect changes in applicable data protection law, changes in the services described in the Services Agreement, or changes in the categories of personal data or data subjects affected by the processing, and the parties agree to cooperate in good faith to negotiate any such updates.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained in this Agreement and the Services Agreement, the parties agree as follows.

1. ROLES OF THE PARTIES

1.1 Controller is the controller and Processor is the processor of the personal data described in Schedule 1.

1.2 For purposes of this Agreement, "personal data," "processing," "data subject," "controller" and "processor" have the meanings given to those terms, or their closest equivalents, under applicable data protection law, including, where applicable, the law of the jurisdictions in which Controller's patients and referring physicians reside.

1.3 "Sub-processor" means any person, other than an employee of Processor, that Processor engages to process personal data on Processor's behalf in connection with the services described in the Services Agreement.

1.4 "Security Incident" means any confirmed accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, personal data processed under this Agreement.

1.5 Each party shall comply with its respective obligations under applicable data protection law in connection with the processing of personal data under this Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement relieves either party of obligations it holds independently under applicable law.

1.6 Controller represents that it has, and will maintain throughout the term of this Agreement, a lawful basis for the collection and processing of the personal data it discloses or makes accessible to Processor, and that its instructions to Processor, including those set out in the Services Agreement, do not require Processor to process personal data in a manner that would violate applicable data protection law.

1.7 Processor represents that it has the organizational capability, including the personnel, processes and technical measures described in Section 7, to perform its obligations as processor under this Agreement, and that it will maintain that capability throughout the term of this Agreement.

2. PROCESSING INSTRUCTIONS

2.1 Processor shall process personal data only on Controller's documented instructions, which are set out in the underlying services agreement and this Agreement, unless otherwise required by applicable law, in which case Processor shall inform Controller of that legal requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits Processor from doing so.

2.2 If Processor considers that an instruction from Controller infringes applicable data protection law, Processor shall promptly inform Controller, and Processor may suspend performance of that instruction until Controller confirms or modifies it in writing.

2.3 Processor shall not process personal data in a manner inconsistent with the instructions given under this Section 2 and shall notify Controller if, in Processor's reasonable opinion, an instruction would require it to act outside the scope of the services described in the Services Agreement.

3. PURPOSE LIMITATION

3.1 Processor shall process personal data only for the purposes described in Schedule 1 and shall not process personal data for its own purposes.

3.2 Processor shall not use personal data processed under this Agreement to develop, train or improve any product, model or service other than as necessary to provide the services described in the Services Agreement to Controller.

3.3 Processor shall ensure that personal data processed under this Agreement is not combined with personal data of other customers of Processor except where strictly necessary for the shared infrastructure on which the multi-tenant Halcyon Engage platform operates, and in that case only in a manner that preserves logical separation between Controller's data and that of other customers.

3.4 If Processor reasonably believes that performing the services described in the Services Agreement in a manner consistent with this Section 3 has become impossible as a result of a change in applicable data protection law, Processor shall promptly notify Controller, and the parties shall discuss in good faith how to modify the services, this Agreement or the Services Agreement to address that change.

4. DATA SUBJECT REQUESTS

4.1 Processor shall promptly notify Controller of any request it receives from an individual to exercise a privacy right, and shall not respond except on Controller's instructions.

4.2 Taking into account the nature of the processing, Processor shall provide Controller with reasonable assistance, through appropriate technical and organizational measures, to enable Controller to respond to requests from individuals seeking to exercise their rights under applicable data protection law, to the extent Controller does not have the means to respond to such requests itself through the Halcyon Engage platform's existing functionality.

4.3 Processor shall direct any individual who contacts Processor directly concerning the processing of that individual's personal data to Controller, unless applicable law requires Processor to respond directly.

4.4 Processor shall maintain a record of any request described in Section 4.1 that it receives, including the date of receipt and the date on which Processor notified Controller, and shall make that record available to Controller on reasonable request.

5. PERSONNEL CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Processor shall ensure that personnel authorized to process personal data are bound by a duty of confidentiality.

5.2 Processor shall limit access to personal data to those personnel who require access to perform the services described in the Services Agreement and shall ensure that such personnel have received appropriate training on the handling of personal data before being granted access.

6. RETURN AND DELETION

6.1 On termination, Processor shall make personal data available for export for thirty (30) days and shall delete personal data, including from backups, within sixty (60) days thereafter.

6.2 Processor shall provide Controller, on request during the export period described in Section 6.1, with a written confirmation identifying the categories of personal data made available for export and the format in which it was made available.

7. SECURITY

7.1 Processor shall maintain technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, consistent with its SOC 2 Type II and ISO/IEC 27001 certifications.

7.2 Those measures include, without limitation, encryption of personal data in transit and at rest, logical access controls based on the principle of least privilege, maintenance of audit logs for systems that process personal data, a documented incident response process, and periodic penetration testing of the production environment by an independent third party.

7.3 Processor shall review its technical and organisational measures at least annually and shall update them as reasonably necessary to address changes in the threat environment or in the nature of the processing described in Schedule 1.

7.4 Processor shall make its then-current SOC 2 Type II report available to Controller on request, subject to a mutually agreed confidentiality undertaking, to allow Controller to evaluate the measures described in this Section 7.

7.5 Processor shall maintain a documented business continuity and disaster recovery plan for the systems used to provide the services described in the Services Agreement, and shall test that plan at least annually.

8. BREACH NOTIFICATION

8.1 Processor shall notify Controller within twenty-four (24) hours after Processor confirms a security incident affecting personal data, describing the incident to the extent then known.

<!-- Breach notice tightened to twenty-four (24) hours from confirmation, at the outer edge of Halcyon's yellow zone. -->

8.2 Processor shall provide Controller with such further information concerning a Security Incident as becomes available to Processor following the notice required under Section 8.1, and shall reasonably cooperate with Controller's own investigation and any regulatory or individual notifications Controller determines are required.

8.3 A notice under this Section 8 is not an admission by Processor of fault or liability with respect to the Security Incident.

8.4 Processor shall designate a point of contact for notices under this Section 8 and shall keep that designation current throughout the term of this Agreement. Notice under this Section 8 may be given to that point of contact notwithstanding the general notice provisions of Section 15.2.

9. SUB-PROCESSORS

9.1 Processor shall not engage any Sub-processor to process personal data under this Agreement without Controller's prior written consent for that specific Sub-processor. Processor shall submit a written request identifying the proposed Sub-processor, the processing it will perform and its data protection safeguards, and shall not proceed until Controller has approved the request in writing.

<!-- Per-sub-processor prior written consent, replacing Halcyon's standard general-authorization model. -->

9.2 Processor shall impose equivalent data protection obligations on each approved Sub-processor and shall remain liable for each Sub-processor's acts and omissions.

9.3 Controller's approval of a Sub-processor under this Section 9 does not relieve Processor of any of its obligations under this Agreement, and Processor shall remain responsible for ensuring that each approved Sub-processor processes personal data consistently with the terms of this Agreement.

9.4 Processor shall maintain a current list of approved Sub-processors and shall make that list available to Controller on request. If Controller does not respond to a written request submitted under Section 9.1 within thirty (30) days, the request is deemed denied, and Processor shall not engage the proposed Sub-processor.

9.5 If Controller withdraws its approval of a Sub-processor that has already been engaged, Processor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to transition the affected processing away from that Sub-processor within a reasonable time, and the parties shall discuss in good faith any additional time or resources required to complete that transition without disrupting the services described in the Services Agreement.

9.6 Processor shall notify Controller promptly if Processor becomes aware that an approved Sub-processor has failed to meet its data protection obligations, and shall describe the steps Processor is taking, or proposes to take, to address that failure.

10. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

10.1 Personal data is hosted in Processor's AWS us-east-1 and us-west-2 regions.

10.2 Processor shall not transfer personal data processed under this Agreement to a storage or processing location outside of the regions identified in Section 10.1 without Controller's prior written consent, except as necessary for Processor's approved Sub-processors to perform the functions described in the applicable approval under Section 9.

11. RETENTION

11.1 Processor may retain personal data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, and in no event longer than is required to comply with applicable law. Where return or destruction of personal data is not feasible, Processor shall extend the protections of this Agreement to the personal data retained and limit further processing to those purposes that make return or destruction infeasible.

12. AUDIT

12.1 Once per year, on thirty (30) days' notice, Controller may request Processor's SOC 2 Type II report and a completed security questionnaire, at Controller's own cost unless the audit reveals a material breach.

12.2 Processor shall respond to a security questionnaire provided under Section 12.1 within thirty (30) days of receipt, and shall make available to Controller a knowledgeable representative to discuss the questionnaire responses on reasonable prior notice.

12.3 If an audit conducted under this Section 12 identifies a material deficiency in Processor's compliance with this Agreement, Processor shall provide Controller with a written remediation plan within thirty (30) days and shall implement that plan within a reasonable time.

13. LIABILITY

13.1 Liability under this Agreement is subject to the limitations of liability set out in the underlying services agreement.

14. GOVERNING LAW

14.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

15. GENERAL PROVISIONS

15.1 This Agreement may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.

15.2 Notices shall be given in writing to each party's address on the signature page, and are deemed received on the date of delivery if delivered by hand or nationally recognized courier, or on confirmed receipt if delivered by email.

15.3 This Agreement, together with the Services Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the processing of personal data described in this Agreement, and supersedes any prior data-protection terms discussed or exchanged between the parties on the subject. In the event of a direct conflict between this Agreement and the Services Agreement with respect to the processing of personal data, this Agreement controls to the extent of that conflict; in all other respects, the Services Agreement controls.

ANNEX 1: DESCRIPTION OF PROCESSING

Categories of data subjects: patients of Controller's laboratory and physician-practice network who use the Halcyon Engage platform to receive appointment reminders, secure messages and intake forms; referring physicians and their administrative staff who access care-coordination features; and Controller's own personnel who administer the Halcyon Engage platform on Controller's behalf.

Categories of personal data: name, date of birth, contact details, appointment and scheduling information, intake-form responses, secure messaging content, and account credentials for the Halcyon Engage platform. Personal data processed under this Agreement does not include laboratory test results or diagnostic images, which are held in Controller's own laboratory information system and are not transmitted to the Halcyon Engage platform.

Nature and purpose of processing: hosting, storage, transmission and display of the categories of personal data described above for the purpose of providing the patient engagement and care coordination functionality described in the Services Agreement, including generating and sending appointment reminders, receiving and routing secure messages, and collecting and storing intake-form responses.

Duration of processing: for the term of the Services Agreement and, with respect to retained personal data, for the periods described in Sections 6 and 11 of this Agreement.

Sub-processors approved as of the effective date of this Agreement:

(a) Processor's cloud infrastructure provider, which hosts the production environment for the Halcyon Engage platform in the regions identified in Section 10.1 and provides the underlying compute, storage and networking services on which the platform runs. This Sub-processor does not have access to personal data other than as necessary to provide hosting infrastructure, and processes personal data only within the regions identified in Section 10.1.

(b) Processor's transactional email and SMS delivery vendor, which transmits appointment reminders and other transactional messages generated by the Halcyon Engage platform to patients and referring physicians on Processor's behalf. This Sub-processor receives only the contact details and message content necessary to deliver a given communication.

(c) Processor's customer support ticketing vendor, which Processor uses to log and track support requests that Controller's administrative users submit concerning the Halcyon Engage platform. This Sub-processor may incidentally receive personal data where a support request describes an issue involving a specific patient record, limited to what is necessary to diagnose and resolve the issue.

Each Sub-processor identified in this Annex was approved by Controller in writing before being engaged, consistent with Section 9.1, and remains subject to the obligations described in Sections 9.2 through 9.6.

Frequency of transfer: continuous, for as long as the Services Agreement remains in effect, reflecting the ongoing nature of the patient engagement functionality described in the Services Agreement.

Volume and recurrence: personal data is created, updated and accessed on an ongoing, transactional basis as patients and referring physicians interact with the Halcyon Engage platform, rather than through periodic bulk transfers.

ANNEX 2: SCHEDULE OF SAFEGUARDS

This Annex describes, at a summary level, the categories of technical and organizational measures referenced in Section 7. The absence of a specific measure from this summary does not relieve Processor of its obligation under Section 7.1 to maintain measures appropriate to the risk.

Access control: role-based access to production systems, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, and periodic access reviews conducted by Processor's security team.

Encryption: encryption of personal data at rest using industry-standard algorithms, and encryption of personal data in transit using current versions of Transport Layer Security.

Logging and monitoring: centralized logging of access to systems that process personal data, automated alerting for anomalous access patterns, and retention of logs for a period sufficient to support incident investigation.

Personnel security: background checks consistent with applicable law for personnel with access to production systems, confidentiality undertakings described in Section 5, and role-specific security training.

Physical security: reliance on the physical security controls of Processor's cloud infrastructure provider, which maintains SOC 2 Type II certification covering physical and environmental controls at its data centers.

Business continuity: the plan described in Section 7.5, including defined recovery time and recovery point objectives for the production environment supporting the Halcyon Engage platform.

Vulnerability management: a documented patch management process for production systems, routine vulnerability scanning of the production environment, and the annual third-party penetration test described in Section 7.2, with findings tracked to remediation on a schedule proportionate to severity.

Data segregation: the logical separation measures described in Section 3.3, implemented through tenant-scoped data stores and access controls that prevent one customer's personal data from being accessible through another customer's account on the Halcyon Engage platform.

Change management: a documented process for reviewing and approving changes to production systems before deployment, including a rollback procedure, to reduce the risk that a change to the Halcyon Engage platform compromises the safeguards described in this Annex.
