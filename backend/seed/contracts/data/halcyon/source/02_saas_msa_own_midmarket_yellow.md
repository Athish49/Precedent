MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of April 14, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Halcyon Care, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business in Boston, Massachusetts ("Halcyon"), and Lakeshore Regional Health Group, a regional physician network organized under the laws of Wisconsin ("Customer"). Halcyon and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

This Agreement is based on Halcyon's standard template, as negotiated and marked up by Customer's procurement team.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Halcyon has developed and operates Halcyon Engage, a multi-tenant software-as-a-service platform for patient engagement and care coordination;

WHEREAS, Customer is a regional physician network that wishes to subscribe to the Services for use by its personnel and affiliated providers, and Customer's procurement team has reviewed and marked up Halcyon's standard template, resulting in the departures from Halcyon's standard terms identified in this Agreement; and

WHEREAS, Halcyon and Customer wish to set out the terms on which Halcyon will make the Services available to Customer, with specific Services, Fees and Subscription Term for each engagement to be set out in one or more Order Forms incorporating this Agreement by reference; and

WHEREAS, Customer's procurement team conducted a competitive review of patient engagement platforms before selecting Halcyon Engage, and the Parties negotiated this Agreement with the assistance of counsel for each Party;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Documentation" means Halcyon's then-current user guides and specifications for the Services, as updated from time to time.

1.2 "Customer Data" means electronic data submitted by Customer or its Authorized Users to the Services.

1.3 "Order Form" means an order document referencing this Agreement and specifying the Services subscribed, the Fees and the Subscription Term.

1.4 "Fees" means the amounts payable by Customer under an Order Form.

1.5 "Services" means Halcyon Engage, Halcyon's patient engagement and care coordination software, as further described in the Documentation.

1.6 "Subscription Term" means the Initial Term and each Renewal Term.

1.7 "Affiliate" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party.

1.8 "Authorized Users" means Customer's employees, contractors and affiliated healthcare providers who are authorized by Customer to access and use the Services.

1.9 "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure, including the terms of this Agreement and Customer Data.

1.10 "Breach" means the acquisition, access, use or disclosure of Customer Data in a manner not permitted by this Agreement that compromises the security, confidentiality or integrity of that data.

1.11 "Malicious Code" means any code, file or program designed to disrupt, damage or gain unauthorized access to a computer system or network, including viruses, worms, time bombs and Trojan horses.

1.12 "Applicable Law" means the laws, regulations and binding regulatory guidance applicable to a Party's performance of its obligations under this Agreement.

1.13 "Scheduled Maintenance" means planned downtime of the Services for upgrades, patches or infrastructure work for which Halcyon gives Customer advance notice in accordance with the Documentation.

2. SERVICES

2.1 Subject to this Agreement and the applicable Order Form, Halcyon shall make the Services available to Customer during the Subscription Term. Halcyon hosts the Services on infrastructure located in AWS `us-east-1` and `us-west-2`, consistent with Halcyon's standard hosting regions for its United States customers.

2.2 Halcyon warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation when used as intended. Customer's exclusive remedy for a breach of this warranty is correction of the non-conformity or a pro-rated refund.

2.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN SECTION 2.2, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS." HALCYON DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE AND NON-INFRINGEMENT.

2.4 Halcyon will provide Customer with access credentials for a reasonable number of Authorized Users as specified in the applicable Order Form. Customer may add Authorized Users during the Subscription Term, subject to any additional Fees set out in the Order Form or a mutually agreed amendment.

2.5 Customer shall not, and shall ensure that its Authorized Users do not: (a) reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Services; (b) use the Services to transmit malicious code; or (c) use the Services in a way that interferes with Halcyon's provision of the Services to other customers.

2.6 Each Party represents and warrants to the other that it is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of its jurisdiction of organization, and that it has full corporate power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Customer shall pay the Fees set out in each Order Form. Invoices are issued annually in advance. Notwithstanding Halcyon's standard Net 30 terms, Customer, as a regional health network with a fixed sixty-day accounts payable cycle, shall pay each invoice within sixty (60) days of the invoice date ("Net 60").

3.2 Amounts not paid when due accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the highest rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

3.3 Halcyon may increase the Fees upon each renewal by notice at least sixty (60) days before the renewal date, provided the increase does not exceed seven percent (7%) of the prior year's Fees.

3.4 Fees are exclusive of taxes. Customer is responsible for all sales, use, value added and similar taxes other than taxes on Halcyon's net income.

4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 The initial term of each Order Form is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date (the "Initial Term"), unless otherwise stated in the Order Form.

4.2 Following the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews for successive twelve (12)-month terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

4.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for the other Party's material breach if the breach remains uncured fifteen (15) days after written notice describing the breach.

<!-- Deviation 2 of 4: cure period shortened from Halcyon's standard thirty (30) days to fifteen (15) days. -->

4.4 Upon termination or expiration, Customer's right to access the Services ceases, all accrued and unpaid Fees become due, and Halcyon shall make Customer Data available for export for thirty (30) days following the effective date of termination.

5. SUSPENSION

5.1 Halcyon may suspend Customer's access to the Services for non-payment that remains uncured for ten (10) business days after notice, or immediately where Halcyon reasonably believes suspension is necessary to prevent harm to the Services or to other customers.

5.2 Where Halcyon suspends the Services under this Section 5, Halcyon will use commercially reasonable efforts to notify Customer's designated contacts of the suspension and the reason for it, and will restore access promptly once the condition giving rise to the suspension has been resolved.

5.3 A suspension under this Section 5 does not relieve Customer of its obligation to pay Fees that accrued before or during the suspension.

6. SERVICE LEVELS

6.1 Halcyon will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.9%, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and events of force majeure.

6.2 If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below 99.9% in a calendar month, Customer's sole and exclusive remedy is a service credit calculated on a sliding scale, capped at ten percent (10%) of the Fees payable for the affected month.

6.3 Customer must request any service credit in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the calendar month to which the credit relates, specifying the dates and duration of the claimed unavailability, as further described in the Documentation.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party shall protect the other Party's Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, but no less than reasonable care.

7.2 Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was rightfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

7.3 A Party may disclose the other Party's Confidential Information to the extent required by law or a valid order of a court or governmental authority, provided that, where legally permitted, it gives the other Party reasonable advance notice.

8. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

8.1 Halcyon shall use Customer Data solely to provide the Services to Customer, and, in de-identified and aggregated form, to improve the Services. Halcyon shall not sell Customer Data or use it for advertising.

8.2 Halcyon maintains a written information security program with administrative, technical and physical safeguards consistent with its SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001 and HITRUST r2 certifications.

8.3 Halcyon shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of confirming a Breach of Customer Data.

8.4 Halcyon may engage Sub-processors under its general written authorization. Notwithstanding Halcyon's standard thirty (30)-day notice period, Halcyon will give Customer at least forty-five (45) days' notice before engaging a new Sub-processor, reflecting Customer's internal vendor-review cycle, and Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within fifteen (15) days of notice.

9. AUDIT

9.1 No more than once in any twelve-month period, and on thirty (30) days' prior written notice, Customer may request Halcyon's then-current SOC 2 Type II report and a written security questionnaire response in place of an on-site audit.

9.2 Any report or questionnaire response provided under this Section 9 is Halcyon's Confidential Information, and Customer shall use it solely to assess Halcyon's compliance with this Agreement.

10. COMPLIANCE

10.1 Each Party shall comply with the laws applicable to it in performing this Agreement.

11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 Except for the Excluded Claims, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the Fees paid and payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the first event giving rise to liability.

11.2 The limitation in Section 11.1 does not apply to: (a) either Party's fraud or wilful misconduct; (b) Customer's payment obligations; and (c) Halcyon's indemnification obligations under Section 13.

11.3 Notwithstanding Section 11.1, Halcyon's aggregate liability for a Breach of the data protection obligations in Section 8 shall not exceed two (2) times the annual Fees paid by Customer in the preceding twelve months.

11.4 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOSS OF PROFITS, REVENUE, DATA OR GOODWILL.

12. INDEMNIFICATION

12.1 Halcyon shall defend Customer against any third-party claim that the Services infringe or misappropriate that third party's intellectual property rights, and shall indemnify Customer against damages finally awarded, provided Customer gives prompt notice and allows Halcyon sole control of the defense and settlement.
