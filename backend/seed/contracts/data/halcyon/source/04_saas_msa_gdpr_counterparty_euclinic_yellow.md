SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of February 20, 2026, between Clairvoie Clinique Group SA, a clinic network incorporated in France ("Customer"), and Halcyon Care Ireland Ltd, a private company incorporated in Ireland ("Halcyon"). This Agreement is drafted on Customer's standard French-law vendor template, as adapted for this transaction.

Customer operates a network of outpatient clinics across metropolitan France and wishes to deploy Halcyon's patient engagement and care coordination platform, known as Halcyon Engage, to support appointment reminders, secure messaging with patients, electronic intake forms and care-plan tracking across its clinic network. Halcyon is in the business of providing such software as a multi-tenant, subscription-based service to hospital systems, physician groups and clinic networks, and has agreed to provide the Services to Customer on the terms of this Agreement, the accompanying Order Form, and the Data Processing Addendum attached as Exhibit B. The parties intend this Agreement, together with its exhibits, to be the complete and exclusive statement of their agreement concerning the Services.

Customer selected Halcyon Engage following a procurement process in which Customer evaluated the platform's ability to integrate with Customer's existing hospital record systems over HL7 FHIR, its support for French-language clinical workflows, and Halcyon's certifications and data residency commitments relevant to the processing of personal data of patients and staff located in France. Each party has the full corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations under it, and the individual signing this Agreement on behalf of each party is duly authorized to do so.
