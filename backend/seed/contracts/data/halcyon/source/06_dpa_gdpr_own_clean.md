DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "Addendum") is entered into as of March 30, 2026, by and between Halcyon Care Ireland Ltd ("Processor") and Solheim Clinic Network AB, a clinic operator organized under the laws of Sweden ("Controller"), and supplements the Master Subscription Agreement between the parties dated March 30, 2026 (the "Agreement"). This Addendum is Halcyon's standard template for EU customers.

Controller operates a network of private clinics in Sweden and has subscribed to the Halcyon Engage platform under the Agreement to support appointment reminders, secure patient messaging, electronic intake forms and care-plan tracking across its clinics. In the course of providing the Services, Processor processes personal data of Controller's patients and staff on Controller's behalf. This Addendum reflects Halcyon's standard terms for processing personal data on behalf of EU customers and is designed to meet the requirements of Article 28 of the GDPR. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Addendum have the meanings given in the Agreement.

The parties have agreed this Addendum in connection with the Agreement so that Processor's processing of Personal Data on Controller's behalf is governed by a written contract that meets the requirements of Article 28(3) of the GDPR, including the matters required to be addressed in that contract: the subject matter and duration of the processing, the nature and purpose of the processing, the type of Personal Data and categories of Data Subjects, and the obligations and rights of Controller. This Addendum applies for as long as Processor processes Personal Data on behalf of Controller in connection with the Services, including during any period after expiry or termination of the Agreement in which Processor retains Personal Data under Section 11.

0. DEFINITIONS

0.1 "GDPR" means Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (the General Data Protection Regulation), as amended, and, where applicable, the UK General Data Protection Regulation as it forms part of the law of the United Kingdom.

0.2 "Personal Data", "Processing", "Controller", "Processor", "Data Subject" and "Supervisory Authority" have the meanings given in the GDPR.

0.3 "Standard Contractual Clauses" means the standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to third countries approved by the European Commission, as they may be amended, replaced or superseded from time to time.

0.4 "Personal Data Breach" has the meaning given in the GDPR and includes the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data processed under this Addendum.

0.5 "Sub-processor" means any processor engaged by Processor to process Personal Data on behalf of Controller in connection with the Services, including Processor's Affiliates where they process Personal Data in that capacity.

0.6 "Service Personal Data" means the Personal Data described in Annex 1 that Processor processes on behalf of Controller in connection with the Services.

1. ROLES OF THE PARTIES

1.1 For the purposes of the GDPR, Controller is the controller and Processor is the processor of the Personal Data described in Annex 1. Processor acts as an independent controller only in respect of account administration and billing data relating to Controller's own personnel.

1.2 This Addendum applies to the Processing of Service Personal Data by Processor and its Sub-processors in connection with the Services, and does not apply to Processing for which Processor is itself a controller, which is addressed in Processor's privacy notice for its own personnel and billing contacts.

1.3 Where Controller instructs Processor to engage with a third-party system on Controller's behalf, such as Controller's own hospital record system accessed through the integration described in the Agreement, Controller remains the controller of the Personal Data held in that third-party system, and this Addendum applies only to Processor's own Processing of Service Personal Data within the Services.

2. SUBJECT MATTER AND INSTRUCTIONS

2.1 Processor shall process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of Controller, including with regard to transfers, unless required to do otherwise by applicable law. The Agreement and this Addendum, together with Annex 1, constitute the complete instructions of Controller as of the effective date of this Addendum.

2.2 Processor shall promptly inform Controller if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other applicable data protection law.

2.3 Processor shall process Personal Data only for the subject matter, duration, nature and purpose described in Annex 1, and shall not process Personal Data for its own purposes.

2.4 Controller may give Processor additional instructions consistent with the Agreement and this Addendum during the term of the Agreement, including instructions concerning the configuration of the Services' data retention and access control settings, by using the administrative functionality of the Services or by written notice to Processor.

2.5 If following an instruction under this Section 2 would require Processor to incur material additional cost or to make a material change to the Services, Processor shall notify Controller before implementing the instruction, and the parties shall discuss in good faith how the instruction will be implemented and, if applicable, funded.

3. DATA SUBJECT REQUESTS

3.1 Processor shall promptly notify Controller of any request received directly from a Data Subject to exercise rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection, and shall not respond to such a request except on Controller's instructions.

3.2 Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to Controller, using appropriate technical and organisational measures, to help Controller fulfil its obligation to respond to Data Subjects within the time limits set by the GDPR.

3.3 Where the Services include self-service functionality that allows Controller to respond to a Data Subject request directly, such as tools to export, rectify or delete a Data Subject's data within the Services, Processor's assistance obligation under Section 3.2 is satisfied by making that functionality available, except where Controller reasonably requires additional assistance that the functionality does not provide.

4. PERSONNEL

4.1 Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality and access Personal Data only as needed for their role.

4.2 Processor shall provide its personnel who have access to Personal Data with training on data protection and information security appropriate to their role, at least annually, and shall maintain records evidencing that training.

4.3 Processor shall take reasonable steps to ensure the reliability of its personnel who have access to Personal Data, consistent with its standard hiring practices, and shall promptly revoke a person's access to Personal Data when that person's role no longer requires it, including on termination of that person's employment or engagement.

5. SECURITY

5.1 Processor shall implement and maintain the technical and organisational measures described in the Security Annex attached as Annex 2, which are designed to protect Service Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access, and are consistent with Processor's SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 and HITRUST r2 certifications. Processor may update the measures described in Annex 2 from time to time, provided that any such update does not materially reduce the overall level of protection for Service Personal Data.

5.2 Processor shall, on request and no more than once in any twelve-month period, provide Controller with a summary of material changes made to the measures described in Annex 2 since the last such summary.

6. DATA PROTECTION IMPACT ASSESSMENTS

6.1 Processor shall provide Controller with reasonable assistance, taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to Processor, with any data protection impact assessment and any prior consultation of a supervisory authority.

6.2 Processor's assistance under Section 6.1 may be provided by making available to Controller the Documentation, Processor's SOC 2 Type II report, and written responses to Controller's reasonable questions about the Processing described in Annex 1.

6.3 Where Controller's data protection impact assessment identifies a residual risk that Controller reasonably considers requires an additional measure beyond those described in Annex 2, the parties shall discuss in good faith whether such a measure can be implemented, and on what timeline, having regard to Processor's standard security roadmap and release process.

7. BREACH NOTIFICATION

7.1 Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of confirming a Personal Data Breach, and shall provide the information described in Annex 3 as it becomes available, including by phased notice where complete information is not yet available.

7.2 Processor shall cooperate with Controller and take reasonable commercial steps as are necessary to assist in the investigation, mitigation and remediation of a Personal Data Breach, including providing updates as material new information becomes available until the Personal Data Breach has been resolved.

8. SUB-PROCESSORS

8.1 Controller gives Processor general written authorisation to engage Sub-processors from the list published at trust.halcyon.com/subprocessors. Processor shall notify Controller of any new Sub-processor at least thirty (30) days before it begins processing Personal Data, and Controller may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within fifteen (15) days of that notice.

8.2 Processor shall impose on each Sub-processor, by written contract, data protection obligations no less protective than those in this Addendum, and shall remain liable to Controller for each Sub-processor's performance.

8.3 The Sub-processors currently engaged by Processor in connection with the Services include providers of cloud hosting infrastructure, customer support ticketing, and transactional email delivery, each of which is identified by name and processing location on the list referenced in Section 8.1 and is subject to a data processing agreement consistent with Section 8.2.

8.4 If Controller objects to a new Sub-processor under Section 8.1 on reasonable data-protection grounds and the parties are unable to resolve the objection, Processor may, as its sole obligation, either not engage the Sub-processor to process Service Personal Data or offer Controller the option to terminate the affected Services without penalty, subject to the termination provisions of the Agreement.

8.5 Processor shall keep the list referenced in Section 8.1 current and shall make it available to Controller through Processor's trust centre at all times during the term of the Agreement.

9. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

9.1 Personal Data relating to Controller's EU and UK operations is hosted in Halcyon's AWS eu-west-1 region on a multi-tenant basis. Any transfer of Personal Data outside the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom shall be made subject to the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another valid transfer mechanism.

9.2 Where Processor relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses for a transfer described in Section 9.1, the parties adopt Module Two (controller to processor) of the Standard Contractual Clauses, which are incorporated into this Addendum by reference, with Annex 1 to this Addendum serving as the corresponding annex to the Standard Contractual Clauses to the extent consistent with its content.

9.3 Processor shall make available to Controller the information reasonably necessary for Controller to assess whether the Standard Contractual Clauses provide an adequate level of protection for a given transfer, including information about the countries in which Sub-processors identified under Section 8 process Service Personal Data.

9.4 Processor will notify Controller if Processor becomes aware that it can no longer comply with the Standard Contractual Clauses incorporated under Section 9.2, in which case the parties will cooperate in good faith to identify an alternative transfer mechanism or, where necessary, to suspend the affected transfer until an adequate mechanism is in place.

10. AUDIT AND COOPERATION

10.1 No more than once in any twelve-month period, on thirty (30) days' prior written notice, Controller may request Processor's SOC 2 Type II report and a written response to a security questionnaire in place of an on-site audit, at Controller's own cost.

10.2 Processor shall cooperate on request with a supervisory authority's enquiries and shall notify Controller of any enquiry, investigation or request from a supervisory authority concerning the Personal Data processed under this Addendum, unless prohibited by law from doing so.

10.3 If a request under Section 10.1 for a security questionnaire response is not sufficient for Controller to satisfy a specific, documented regulatory requirement, Controller may request additional written information from Processor, which Processor shall provide within a reasonable time, having regard to the nature of the information requested.

10.4 Processor shall maintain, and make available to Controller on request, records sufficient to demonstrate Processor's compliance with this Addendum, including the measures described in Annex 2, Processor's certifications described in Annex 2, and the list of Sub-processors described in Section 8.

11. RETURN AND DELETION

11.1 On termination of the Agreement, Processor shall make Personal Data available for export for thirty (30) days and shall thereafter securely delete Personal Data from production systems within sixty (60) days, including from backup copies as they are cycled out.

11.2 Processor shall, on Controller's written request made within the thirty (30) day period described in Section 11.1, provide Controller with written confirmation once the deletion described in that Section has been completed.

11.3 Sections 7 (Breach Notification), 8.2 (Sub-processor liability), 9 (International Transfers), 11 (Return and Deletion) and 12 (Liability and Governing Law) survive termination or expiry of the Agreement for so long as Processor retains any Personal Data under Section 11.1.

12. LIABILITY AND GOVERNING LAW

12.1 Liability under this Addendum is subject to the limitations of liability set out in the Agreement, taken together in the aggregate with any liability under the Agreement.

12.2 This Addendum is governed by the laws of Ireland.

13. COMPLIANCE WITH DATA PROTECTION LAWS

13.1 Each party shall comply with its respective obligations under the GDPR, including Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and, where applicable, the UK GDPR, in connection with the Processing described in this Addendum. Controller is solely responsible for establishing the lawful basis for its Processing of Personal Data and for providing any notices to Data Subjects required under Articles 13 and 14 of the GDPR, and Processor is responsible for Processing Service Personal Data in accordance with Controller's documented instructions and this Addendum.

14. RECORDS OF PROCESSING

14.1 Processor maintains a written record of the categories of Processing it carries out on behalf of its customers, consistent with Article 30(2) of the GDPR, which describes the categories of Processing, the categories of Personal Data and Data Subjects, and the safeguards applicable to transfers, and which is referenced by the Documentation and by Processor's SOC 2 Type II report. Processor will make a summary of the record applicable to the Processing described in Annex 1 available to Controller within ten (10) business days of a written request.

15. GENERAL PROVISIONS

15.1 This Addendum, together with the Agreement and its Annexes, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the Processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, and supersedes any prior data processing terms between the parties concerning the Services.

15.2 If there is a conflict between this Addendum and the Agreement concerning the Processing of Personal Data, this Addendum prevails to the extent of the conflict.

15.3 Headings in this Addendum are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation. References to a statute include its amendments and successor legislation, and "including" means "including without limitation".

15.4 Neither party may assign this Addendum except together with the Agreement, and in accordance with the assignment provisions of the Agreement.

15.5 This Addendum may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original.

15.6 If any provision of this Addendum is held invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be limited or eliminated to the minimum extent necessary so that this Addendum otherwise remains in full force and effect.

15.7 This Addendum is for the sole benefit of the parties, except that, to the extent required for the Standard Contractual Clauses incorporated under Section 9.2 to be enforceable, Data Subjects may enforce the third-party beneficiary rights expressly provided for in those Clauses.

15.8 Each party is an independent contractor with respect to the other for the purposes of this Addendum, and nothing in this Addendum creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the parties.

15.9 Notices under this Addendum shall be given in the manner provided for notices under the Agreement, and shall additionally be copied to Processor's data protection officer at the address published at trust.halcyon.com and to Controller's designated privacy contact.

15.10 This Addendum will be updated by the parties as reasonably necessary to reflect changes in the GDPR, guidance issued by a supervisory authority, or a replacement of the Standard Contractual Clauses by the European Commission, and any such update will be agreed in writing and will not reduce the overall level of protection for Service Personal Data below that provided by this Addendum as of its effective date.

ANNEX 1 - DESCRIPTION OF PROCESSING

Categories of Data Subjects: patients of Controller's clinics, and employees, contractors and agents of Controller who use the Services as authorized users.

Categories of Personal Data: patient identification and contact details (name, date of birth, address, telephone number, email address); appointment and scheduling information; secure messaging content exchanged between patients and clinic staff within the Services; electronic intake form responses, which may include medical history and current symptoms; and care-plan tracking data, including care goals, tasks and progress notes recorded by clinic staff. Some of these categories constitute special categories of Personal Data concerning health within the meaning of Article 9 of the GDPR.

Nature and Purpose of Processing: Processor processes Service Personal Data to provide the Services to Controller, including hosting, storage, transmission, display and retrieval of Service Personal Data within the Halcyon Engage platform, generation of appointment reminders, facilitation of secure messaging between patients and clinic staff, collection of intake form responses, tracking of care-plan tasks, and provision of related customer support.

Duration of Processing: for the term of the Agreement, and thereafter for the data retrieval and deletion periods described in Section 11 of this Addendum.

Frequency of Transfer: continuous, for as long as the Services are in use.

Volume and Scale: approximately eighteen thousand (18,000) active patient records and four hundred (400) Authorized User accounts as of the effective date of this Addendum, expected to grow in the ordinary course as Controller's clinic network expands, without a corresponding change to the nature or purpose of Processing described above.

Retention within the Services: Service Personal Data is retained within the Services for the duration of the Agreement in accordance with Controller's configured retention settings, subject to the data retrieval and deletion periods described in Section 11 of this Addendum following termination.

ANNEX 2 - TECHNICAL AND ORGANISATIONAL MEASURES

Access control: role-based access controls limiting access to Service Personal Data to personnel who need it to perform their role, multi-factor authentication for administrative access to production systems, and periodic review of access rights.

Encryption: encryption of Service Personal Data in transit using current industry-standard protocols, and encryption of Service Personal Data at rest in Processor's production databases and backups.

Resilience and availability: redundant infrastructure across multiple availability zones within the AWS eu-west-1 region, automated backups, and a documented disaster recovery plan tested at least annually.

Monitoring and logging: logging and monitoring of access to production systems and Service Personal Data, with automated alerting on anomalous activity, and retention of logs for a period sufficient to support incident investigation.

Vulnerability management: periodic vulnerability scanning and at least annual third-party penetration testing of the production environment supporting the Services, with findings remediated according to their severity.

Incident response: a documented incident response plan, tested periodically, that governs the detection, investigation, containment and notification of a Personal Data Breach consistent with Section 7 of this Addendum.

Organisational measures: mandatory confidentiality undertakings and data protection training for personnel with access to Service Personal Data, a documented information security policy maintained by Processor's security team, and vendor risk assessment of Sub-processors before they are added to the list referenced in Section 8.1.

Data minimisation and pseudonymisation: configuration options within the Services that allow Controller to limit the fields of Service Personal Data collected through intake forms to those Controller determines are necessary for its clinical workflows, and internal use by Processor of pseudonymised identifiers in non-production environments used for testing and quality assurance.

Physical security: Service Personal Data is hosted in AWS data centres, which are subject to AWS's physical security controls, including controlled physical access, environmental controls and redundant power; Processor does not operate its own data centres for the Services.

Certifications: Processor's measures are assessed annually as part of the audits supporting its SOC 2 Type II report, ISO 27001 certification and HITRUST r2 certification, copies or summaries of which are made available to Controller under Section 10.1.

ANNEX 3 - BREACH NOTIFICATION CONTENT

A notification given under Section 7.1 of this Addendum will include, to the extent known at the time of notification and supplemented by phased notice as further information becomes available: a description of the nature of the Personal Data Breach, including, where possible, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and Personal Data records concerned; the name and contact details of Processor's data protection officer or other contact point from whom more information can be obtained; a description of the likely consequences of the Personal Data Breach; and a description of the measures taken or proposed by Processor to address the Personal Data Breach, including, where appropriate, measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects.
