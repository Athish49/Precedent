MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of October 2, 2026, between Cornerstone Analytics Partners, Inc., a data analytics vendor organized under the laws of Massachusetts ("Vendor"), and Halcyon Care, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Halcyon"), under which Vendor will provide analytics tooling used in Halcyon's internal operations. This Agreement is on Vendor's standard terms, lightly negotiated.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Halcyon operates Halcyon Engage, a multi-tenant patient engagement and care coordination platform used by hospital systems and physician groups, and maintains an internal operations function that analyzes usage, performance and account data to support Halcyon's own business decisions;

WHEREAS, Vendor develops and operates an analytics platform that ingests customer-provided data and produces dashboards, reports and other analytical outputs, and has represented to Halcyon that the Vendor's platform, security program and SOC 2 Type II certification are suitable for use with the categories of data Halcyon intends to submit to the Services;

WHEREAS, Halcyon's procurement of the Services followed a negotiation in which Halcyon's commercial counsel and security team reviewed Vendor's standard terms against Halcyon's buy-side contracting standards for vendors that process data originating from Halcyon's own operations, and the parties agreed to a number of modifications to Vendor's standard form to reflect that review, as reflected in the terms below;

WHEREAS, the parties intend that this Agreement sets out the general terms that will govern the Order Form executed concurrently with this Agreement and any additional Order Form the parties may execute from time to time referencing this Agreement;

WHEREAS, Vendor has represented that its platform is used by a number of other healthcare-adjacent customers for internal analytics purposes comparable to Halcyon's intended use, and that its security program, insurance coverage and SOC 2 Type II certification are maintained at a level appropriate to that customer base;

WHEREAS, the parties anticipate that Halcyon's use of the Services may expand over the term of this Agreement to additional internal teams and additional categories of operational data as Halcyon's analytics needs develop, and the parties have structured this Agreement, together with the Order Form and Statement of Work mechanisms described below, to accommodate that anticipated growth without requiring renegotiation of this Agreement's general terms;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth below, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Services" means Vendor's analytics platform. "Halcyon Data" means data Halcyon submits to the Services. "Fees" means the amounts payable under the Order Form.

1.2 "Order Form" means a written or electronic order document, executed or accepted by both parties, that references this Agreement and specifies the Services ordered, the Fees, the Initial Term, and any Service-specific terms. Each Order Form forms part of this Agreement upon execution.

1.3 "Documentation" means Vendor's then-current online user guides, API reference materials and other documentation made generally available to Vendor's customers describing the features and operation of the Services.

1.4 "Authorized Users" means Halcyon's employees and contractors who are authorized by Halcyon to access and use the Services on Halcyon's behalf, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

1.5 "Affiliate" means, with respect to a party, any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of an entity.

1.6 "Confidential Information" has the meaning given in Section 11.

2. SERVICES

2.1 Vendor warrants that the Services will perform substantially as described in Vendor's documentation during the term of this Agreement.

2.2 Vendor shall make the Services available to Halcyon and its Authorized Users for Halcyon's internal business purposes, including the ingestion, processing and visualization of Halcyon Data to produce dashboards, reports and other analytical outputs for Halcyon's internal use, in accordance with this Agreement, the Documentation and the applicable Order Form.

2.3 Vendor shall provide Halcyon with reasonable access to technical support during Vendor's normal business hours for questions concerning the use of the Services, and shall make available to Halcyon the Documentation necessary for Halcyon's Authorized Users to configure and operate the Services as contemplated by this Agreement.

2.4 Halcyon is responsible for the accuracy and lawfulness of the Halcyon Data it submits to the Services and for configuring the Services, including any access permissions among its Authorized Users, in accordance with the Documentation.

2.5 Vendor may update the Services from time to time, including by releasing new features or modifying existing functionality, provided that Vendor shall not materially reduce the core functionality of the Services that Halcyon has ordered without providing Halcyon with reasonable advance notice.

2.6 From time to time, the parties may execute a Statement of Work describing professional services, custom reporting work, or implementation assistance that Vendor will provide in addition to the standard Services, on the terms set out in Section 17.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Halcyon shall pay the Fees within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date.

<!-- Net 45, within Halcyon's buy-side green range. -->

3.2 Vendor shall invoice Halcyon in accordance with the billing frequency stated in the Order Form. Each invoice shall include reasonable supporting detail sufficient for Halcyon to verify the charges against the Order Form.

3.3 If Halcyon in good faith disputes a portion of an invoice, Halcyon shall pay the undisputed portion by the due date and shall notify Vendor of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute, and the parties shall work together in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

3.4 Fees are stated and payable in United States dollars unless the applicable Order Form states otherwise.

4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 This Agreement has an initial term of twenty-four (24) months from the Effective Date, renewing automatically for successive twelve-month terms unless either party gives sixty (60) days' notice of non-renewal.

<!-- 24-month initial term, within Halcyon's buy-side green range. -->

4.2 Either party may terminate for the other party's uncured material breach that remains uncured twenty (20) days after written notice.

<!-- 20-day cure period, within Halcyon's buy-side green range. -->

4.3 Termination of this Agreement does not relieve Halcyon of its obligation to pay Fees accrued before the effective date of termination, and does not limit either party's right to pursue any remedy available to it for the other party's breach.

4.4 A notice of non-renewal or termination under this Section 4 shall be given in writing in accordance with Section 16.5, and is effective upon the other party's receipt unless this Section 4 states a specific notice period, in which case the notice period runs from the date of receipt.

5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 Vendor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available not less than 99.9% of the time each month, excluding scheduled maintenance.

<!-- 99.9% monthly uptime, within Halcyon's buy-side green range. -->

5.2 If Vendor fails to meet the committed uptime, Halcyon's remedy is a service credit calculated on a sliding scale against the Fees for the affected month.

5.3 Scheduled maintenance performed with at least twenty-four (24) hours' advance notice to Halcyon does not count against the uptime commitment in Section 5.1, and Vendor shall use reasonable efforts to schedule maintenance outside of Halcyon's identified peak usage hours where practicable.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

6.1 Vendor's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total Fees paid by Halcyon in the twenty-four (24) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim, except that Vendor's liability for a data breach shall not exceed five (5) times the annual Fees paid by Halcyon.

<!-- 24-month general cap with a 5x data breach super-cap, matching Halcyon's buy-side green position. -->

6.2 In no event shall Vendor be liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, except as to the data breach liability in Section 6.1.

7. INDEMNIFICATION

7.1 Vendor shall defend and indemnify Halcyon against third-party claims that the Services infringe a third party's intellectual property rights.

7.2 Vendor's indemnification obligation under Section 7.1 does not apply to the extent a claim arises from (a) Halcyon's modification of the Services other than as directed by Vendor, (b) Halcyon's use of the Services in combination with software or services not provided by Vendor, where the claim would not have arisen without that combination, or (c) Halcyon's continued use of the Services after Vendor notifies Halcyon of the infringing nature of the Services and makes a non-infringing substitute available at no additional cost.

8. WARRANTIES

8.1 Vendor warrants that the Services will perform substantially in accordance with Vendor's documentation and will be provided in a professional and workmanlike manner.

8.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS AGREEMENT, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED ON AN "AS IS" BASIS, AND VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, WHETHER EXPRESS, IMPLIED OR STATUTORY, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE OR NON-INFRINGEMENT.

8.3 If the Services do not perform substantially in accordance with Section 8.1, Vendor shall correct the non-conforming aspect of the Services within a commercially reasonable time after receiving written notice from Halcyon describing the non-conformance with reasonable specificity.

9. INSURANCE

9.1 Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 and cyber liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000, and shall provide a certificate of insurance on request.

9.2 Vendor shall maintain the insurance required by Section 9.1 for the duration of this Agreement and shall notify Halcyon if any required policy is cancelled or materially reduced in coverage. The insurance required by this Section 9 does not limit Vendor's liability or obligations under this Agreement.

10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

10.1 Vendor retains all right, title and interest in the Services. Halcyon owns all right, title and interest in Halcyon Data.

10.2 Halcyon grants Vendor a limited, non-exclusive license to use Halcyon Data solely to the extent necessary to provide the Services to Halcyon, and for no other purpose.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 Each party shall protect the other's confidential information using the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care.

11.2 For purposes of this Section 11, "confidential information" means non-public information disclosed by one party to the other that is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure or that should reasonably be understood to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure, but excludes information that is public, already known to the receiving party, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party.

11.3 Each party may disclose the other party's confidential information to its own employees, contractors and professional advisers who have a need to know it to perform or receive the Services, provided such persons are bound by confidentiality obligations consistent with this Section 11, and each party remains responsible for their compliance.

11.4 The obligations of confidentiality in this Section 11 do not restrict Vendor's use of aggregated or de-identified data derived from Halcyon Data, provided such data does not identify Halcyon or any individual, and is used by Vendor solely to monitor, maintain and improve the operation of the Services generally across Vendor's customer base.

12. SECURITY

12.1 Vendor shall maintain a written information security program with administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the data it processes, consistent with its SOC 2 Type II certification.

12.2 Vendor's information security program shall address, at a minimum, access controls limiting access to Halcyon Data to personnel who need it to perform the Services, encryption of Halcyon Data both in transit over public networks and at rest, and a process for applying security patches to systems that process Halcyon Data within a reasonable time after a patch becomes available. Vendor shall periodically review and update its information security program to address changes in technology and applicable threats.

12.3 Vendor shall not use Halcyon Data for any purpose other than providing the Services to Halcyon, including training any machine learning or artificial intelligence model that is not dedicated solely to providing the Services to Halcyon, unless Halcyon gives its prior written consent.

13. SECURITY INCIDENTS

13.1 Vendor shall notify Halcyon as soon as practicable, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after discovering a suspected or confirmed security incident affecting Halcyon Data, unless the incident involved no personal data of any kind, in which case Vendor shall notify Halcyon once the incident has been confirmed through its standard review process.

<!-- Notice runs from discovery generally, but from confirmation only when no personal data is involved - a deviation from Halcyon's standard 48-hour, discovery-triggered floor for the limited case of non-personal-data incidents. -->

13.2 A notice given under Section 13.1 shall describe, to the extent then known, the nature of the security incident, the categories and approximate volume of Halcyon Data affected, and the remedial steps Vendor has taken or plans to take, and Vendor shall provide reasonably requested updates as its investigation progresses.

14. SUBCONTRACTORS

14.1 Vendor will provide thirty (30) days' notice before engaging a new subcontractor that will have access to Halcyon Data.

14.2 Vendor remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors as if the subcontractors' acts and omissions were Vendor's own, and shall ensure that any subcontractor with access to Halcyon Data is bound by confidentiality and security obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement.

15. AUDIT

15.1 Once per year, on thirty (30) days' notice, Halcyon may request Vendor's SOC 2 Type II report and a written response to a security questionnaire.

15.2 Vendor shall respond to a request under Section 15.1 within a reasonable time, and shall reasonably cooperate with Halcyon's follow-up questions concerning the SOC 2 Type II report or the security questionnaire response.

16.2 This Agreement may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.

16.3 This Agreement, together with the Order Form, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.

16.4 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.

Vendor's personnel who perform the Services do so as independent contractors of Vendor, and nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between Halcyon and Vendor or any of Vendor's personnel. 16.7 Further Assurances. Each party shall, at the other party's reasonable request and without additional consideration, execute such further documents and take such further actions as are reasonably necessary to give effect to this Agreement.

16.8 No Third-Party Beneficiaries. This Agreement does not create any right in favor of any person who is not a party to it, including any Authorized User or subcontractor, and no such person may enforce any term of this Agreement.

16.9 Severability. If a court of competent jurisdiction holds any provision of this Agreement to be invalid or unenforceable, that provision is modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions of this Agreement remain in full force and effect.

16.10 Interpretation. Section headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. "Including" means "including without limitation." Unless the context requires otherwise, references to this Agreement include its Order Forms and any Statement of Work entered into under Section 17. This Agreement has been negotiated by both parties with the assistance of counsel of their choosing, and no rule of construction resolving ambiguity against the drafting party applies to its interpretation.

16.11 Counterparts; Electronic Signature. This Agreement and each Order Form may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

16.12 Export Control and Trade Compliance. Each party shall comply with applicable export control, economic sanctions and anti-corruption laws in connection with its performance of this Agreement, and shall not provide the Services or any related technology to a person or jurisdiction subject to a denial of U.S. or other applicable export privileges.

16.13 Compliance with Law. Each party shall comply with applicable laws in its performance of this Agreement.

16.14 Records. Each party shall maintain reasonable business records sufficient to support its obligations under this Agreement for the duration of this Agreement and for a reasonable period following its termination or expiration as needed to resolve any dispute concerning amounts invoiced or paid.

16.15 Relationship of the Parties on Data Handling. As between the parties, Halcyon is responsible for the accuracy and lawfulness of the Halcyon Data it submits to the Services, and Vendor is responsible for processing that data in accordance with the Documentation and the safeguards described in Section 12. Neither party's responsibility under this Section 16.15 affects the allocation of liability under Section 6 or the indemnification obligations under Section 7.

Pre-printed or standard terms on an invoice, purchase order or shipping document have no effect on this Agreement.

17.3 Exhibit A to this Agreement sets out an illustrative, sample form of Statement of Work for reference. An actual Statement of Work executed by the parties governs the specific engagement it describes, whether or not it follows the illustrative form in Exhibit A.

EXHIBIT A — SAMPLE STATEMENT OF WORK (ILLUSTRATIVE FORM)

This Exhibit A illustrates the form of Statement of Work the parties may use under Section 17 and is not itself an executed Statement of Work. An actual engagement is governed by the Statement of Work the parties sign for that engagement.

A.1 Description of Services. A Statement of Work typically describes the supplemental services to be performed, such as building a defined set of custom dashboards against Halcyon Data, assisting Halcyon's analytics team with data pipeline configuration, or providing a defined number of hours of analyst support during an initial onboarding period.

A.2 Deliverables and Acceptance. Where a Statement of Work specifies deliverables, Halcyon has a reasonable review period, stated in the Statement of Work, to confirm that a deliverable conforms to its specification before the deliverable is deemed accepted. If Halcyon identifies a material non-conformity within that review period, Vendor shall correct the deliverable and resubmit it for review.

A.3 Fees and Expenses. Fees for services performed under a Statement of Work are set out in that Statement of Work or the related Order Form and are invoiced in accordance with Section 3, unless the Statement of Work specifies a different invoicing schedule, such as invoicing upon completion of defined milestones.

A.4 Term. A Statement of Work remains in effect for the period stated in it, or, if no period is stated, until the described services are completed, and expires automatically upon completion of the described services unless extended by written agreement of the parties.

A.5 Personnel. Vendor shall assign personnel with suitable skill and experience to perform services under a Statement of Work, and may replace assigned personnel in the ordinary course of its business, provided any replacement has comparable qualifications.

A.6 Illustrative Example. By way of illustration only, a Statement of Work for an initial onboarding engagement might describe: a defined number of hours of implementation assistance to configure Halcyon's data feeds and dashboard templates within the Services; a joint validation period, typically two to four weeks, during which Halcyon's analytics team and Vendor's technical account manager confirm that Vendor's reports reconcile against Halcyon's own source data; and a fixed professional services fee payable upon completion of the validation period, distinct from the recurring Fees payable for the standard Services under the Order Form. None of the figures in this Section A.6 are binding, and an actual Statement of Work may adopt a different structure entirely.

A.7 Relationship to the Order Form. Where a Statement of Work and an Order Form both relate to the same engagement, the Order Form governs the ongoing Fees for the standard Services, and the Statement of Work governs the scope and fees for the supplemental services described in it, unless the documents state otherwise.

A.8 Change Requests. If Halcyon requests a change to the scope of a Statement of Work after it has been signed, Vendor shall provide Halcyon with a written estimate of the additional time and fees, if any, associated with the requested change before performing the changed scope, and the change becomes effective only once both parties agree to it in writing.
