MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of August 11, 2026, between Omnivault Technologies, Inc., a large enterprise software company organized under the laws of California ("Omnivault"), and Halcyon Care, Inc. ("Halcyon"), in connection with a potential integration between Halcyon Engage and Omnivault's platform (the "Purpose"). This Agreement is on Omnivault's standard form, used company-wide for its technology partner discussions.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Halcyon develops and operates Halcyon Engage, a patient engagement and care coordination platform used by hospital systems and physician groups to manage appointment reminders, secure messaging, intake forms and care-plan tracking, and integrates that platform with hospital record systems over HL7 FHIR interfaces;

WHEREAS, Omnivault develops and operates an enterprise software platform that Halcyon wishes to evaluate for potential integration with Halcyon Engage, with a view to determining whether a joint technical architecture, a referral relationship, or a combined commercial offering would be of mutual benefit to the parties and to the hospital systems and physician groups that constitute Halcyon's customer base;

WHEREAS, in order to evaluate the Purpose, each party anticipates that it may need to disclose to the other certain non-public information concerning its technology, roadmap, architecture, pricing, customer relationships and business plans, and each party wishes to protect the confidentiality of that information while permitting a sufficiently candid exchange to allow the parties to evaluate the Purpose;

WHEREAS, the parties intend that any disclosure of Confidential Information under this Agreement is made solely to further the Purpose, and that neither party is obligated by this Agreement to proceed with the Purpose, to disclose any particular information, or to enter into any further agreement, and either party may, in its sole discretion, terminate discussions regarding the Purpose at any time, with or without cause, without liability other than as expressly provided in this Agreement;

WHEREAS, Omnivault's platform is used by a range of enterprise customers across several industries for workflow automation, system integration and data orchestration, and Omnivault has expressed interest in understanding how a connector or integration layer built to Halcyon Engage's HL7 FHIR interfaces might extend Omnivault's platform into the health system and physician group market that Halcyon currently serves;

WHEREAS, Halcyon's evaluation of the Purpose will typically proceed through several stages, including an initial technical scoping discussion between the parties' engineering teams, a review of each party's relevant architecture and security documentation, one or more product demonstrations, and, if the parties determine that a closer relationship is warranted, discussion of indicative commercial terms; the parties anticipate that Confidential Information may be exchanged at any or all of these stages, and this Agreement is intended to apply to all such exchanges for so long as they remain directed at the Purpose;

WHEREAS, each party recognizes that the discussions contemplated by the Purpose are exploratory, that the parties may conclude at any stage that the Purpose does not warrant further investment of time or resources, and that this Agreement is not intended to, and does not, obligate either party to continue those discussions, to disclose any particular category of information, or to enter into a commercial, licensing or other definitive agreement with the other party;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth below, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Confidential Information" means information disclosed by one party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party") that is marked or identified in writing as confidential at the time of disclosure, or, if disclosed orally, is confirmed in writing as confidential within thirty (30) days of disclosure. Information not so marked or confirmed is not Confidential Information under this Agreement.

<!-- Marking requirement, departing from Halcyon's standard no-marking-required position. -->

1.2 "Affiliate" means, with respect to a party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities or equivalent voting interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.3 "Representatives" means, with respect to a party, that party's directors, officers, employees, Affiliates, contractors and professional advisers who have a need to know Confidential Information for the Purpose.

1.4 "Purpose" has the meaning given in the preamble above, and includes the parties' technical, commercial and legal evaluation of a potential integration, referral relationship or combined offering involving Halcyon Engage and Omnivault's platform, together with any due diligence, demonstrations, test environments and working sessions reasonably undertaken in furtherance of that evaluation.

1.5 "Disclosing Party" and "Receiving Party" are used in this Agreement with respect to a particular item of Confidential Information, and a party may act as both Disclosing Party and Receiving Party under this Agreement with respect to different items of information exchanged in connection with the Purpose, in which case this Agreement applies separately to each party in each capacity.

1.6 "Agreement" means this Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement, including its recitals and Schedule A, as it may be amended from time to time in accordance with Section 12.1.

1.7 "Effective Date" means the date first written above, regardless of the date on which either party actually countersigns this Agreement, unless the signature page states a later effective date.

1.8 Without limiting Section 1.1, the parties anticipate that Confidential Information disclosed in connection with the Purpose may include, by way of illustration and not limitation, technical documentation, system architecture diagrams, source code excerpts shared for a specific demonstration, product roadmaps, pricing and packaging information, customer lists and customer feedback shared on a de-identified basis, and business plans relating to the Purpose, in each case to the extent the information otherwise satisfies the marking or written-confirmation requirement in Section 1.1.

2. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

2.1 The Receiving Party shall use reasonable care to protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

<!-- Reasonable-care-only standard, without Halcyon's standard own-practice comparator. -->

2.2 The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and shall not use Confidential Information for any other purpose, including developing a competing product or service, without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent.

2.3 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its Representatives who have a need to know such information for the Purpose, provided that each Representative is made aware of the confidential nature of the information and is bound by confidentiality obligations consistent with this Agreement, whether by policy, employment agreement or a separate confidentiality agreement, and the Receiving Party remains responsible for its Representatives' compliance with this Agreement.

2.4 The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any software, prototype or other tangible embodiment of Confidential Information made available to it for the Purpose, except to the extent such restriction is prohibited by applicable law.

2.5 The Receiving Party shall promptly notify the Disclosing Party upon becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, and shall reasonably cooperate with the Disclosing Party, at the Disclosing Party's expense, to help the Disclosing Party regain possession of the Confidential Information and prevent its further unauthorized use or disclosure. This Section 2.5 does not, by itself, require the Receiving Party to take any action that would be unreasonably burdensome or that would require the Receiving Party to disclose its own confidential information to a third party.

3. EXCLUSIONS

3.1 Confidential Information does not include information that is public, already known, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party.

3.2 For the avoidance of doubt, the exclusions in Section 3.1 apply regardless of whether the Receiving Party learns of the exclusion before or after the Disclosing Party discloses the information, and a combination of individually excluded elements does not become Confidential Information merely because the particular combination has not been published.

3.3 Information disclosed under this Agreement that also constitutes Confidential Information under a separate written agreement between the parties remains subject to the terms of that separate agreement to the extent they are more protective of the Disclosing Party than this Agreement, and this Agreement does not narrow any protection available under such a separate agreement.

4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

4.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, provided it gives prior notice where legally permitted.

4.2 Where prior notice is given under Section 4.1, the Receiving Party shall, at the Disclosing Party's request and expense, reasonably cooperate with the Disclosing Party's efforts to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy, and shall disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that its legal counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed.

4.3 A disclosure compelled under this Section 4 does not, by itself, cause the disclosed information to lose its status as Confidential Information for purposes of this Agreement, except to the extent the information becomes public as a result of that disclosure.

4.4 For purposes of this Section 4, "required by law or court order" includes a valid subpoena, civil investigative demand, or other compulsory process issued by a court, arbitral tribunal, administrative agency or legislative body of competent jurisdiction, and a request from a securities, antitrust or similar regulator with authority over the Receiving Party in connection with a routine or non-routine examination, provided that, where the request originates from a regulator with ongoing supervisory authority over the Receiving Party, the notice requirement in Section 4.1 applies only to the extent the Receiving Party is legally permitted to give it without violating the regulator's own confidentiality requirements.

5. RESIDUALS

5.1 Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement, either party's personnel who have had access to the other party's Confidential Information may use, for any purpose, general knowledge, skills and experience, including ideas, concepts, know-how and techniques, retained in the unaided memory of such personnel without reference to any tangible copy of the Confidential Information ("Residuals"). This Section 5 applies to all categories of Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement, without limitation.

5.2 The Residuals right in Section 5.1 does not grant either party a license under the other party's patents or copyrights, and does not relieve either party of its obligations of confidentiality with respect to any Confidential Information retained in tangible or recorded form.

6. EQUITABLE RELIEF

6.1 The parties acknowledge that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which damages would be inadequate, entitling the Disclosing Party to seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies.

6.2 The remedies described in Section 6.1 are cumulative and in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other remedies available to the Disclosing Party at law or in equity, and the Disclosing Party is not required to post a bond or other security as a condition of seeking injunctive relief under this Section 6, except as a court of competent jurisdiction may otherwise require.

7. NO LICENSE

7.1 Except for the Residuals right in Section 5, nothing in this Agreement grants either party a licence in the other's intellectual property.

7.2 All Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement remains the property of the Disclosing Party, and no Receiving Party acquires any right, title or interest in any Confidential Information, patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret or other intellectual property right of the Disclosing Party by virtue of this Agreement or any disclosure made under it.

8. TERM AND SURVIVAL

8.1 This Agreement remains in effect for three (3) years from the Effective Date.

8.2 The confidentiality obligations in this Agreement, and all restrictions on the use of Confidential Information of every kind, survive perpetually and do not terminate at any time, regardless of whether the information constitutes a trade secret.

9. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

9.1 Upon request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, except for archival copies kept in routine backups or as required by law.

9.2 Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall certify in writing that it has complied with Section 9.1, and any archival copies retained under the exception in Section 9.1 remain subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement for as long as they are retained.

9.3 A request under Section 9.1 may be made by either party at any time, including before the expiration of the term stated in Section 8.1, and is not conditioned on the discussions regarding the Purpose having concluded. The Receiving Party shall use reasonable efforts to comply with a request under Section 9.1 within thirty (30) days, except with respect to information stored in the Receiving Party's routine, system-generated backups, which the Receiving Party may retain until those backups are deleted or overwritten in the ordinary course, provided such retained information remains subject to this Agreement.

10. PUBLICITY

10.1 Neither party may use the other's name or logo without prior written consent.

10.2 Nothing in this Section 10 prevents a party from disclosing the existence of discussions regarding the Purpose to its own Representatives on a need-to-know basis, or from making a disclosure otherwise permitted under Section 4.

10.3 A request for consent under Section 10.1 shall be addressed to the other party's primary point of contact for the Purpose, shall describe the proposed use of the requesting party's name or logo with reasonable specificity, including the context and the materials in which it would appear, and shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed by the party whose consent is sought, provided that nothing in this Section 10.3 requires either party to grant such consent.

11. GOVERNING LAW

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

The individual signing this Agreement on behalf of each party represents to the other party that they hold the corporate authority necessary to execute this Agreement and to bind the party on whose behalf they are signing, and that no further internal approval is required for that party's execution of this Agreement.
