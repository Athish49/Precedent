MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of March 3, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Halcyon Care, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business in Boston, Massachusetts ("Halcyon"), and Meridian Physician Partners, LLC, a physician group organized under the laws of Ohio ("Customer"). Halcyon and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Halcyon has developed and operates Halcyon Engage, a multi-tenant software-as-a-service platform for patient engagement and care coordination, including appointment reminders, secure messaging, intake forms and care-plan tracking, and integrates with hospital record systems over HL7 FHIR;

WHEREAS, Customer is a physician group that wishes to subscribe to the Services for use by its personnel and affiliated providers in connection with Customer's delivery of patient care; and

WHEREAS, Halcyon and Customer wish to set out the terms on which Halcyon will make the Services available to Customer, with specific Services, Fees and Subscription Term for each engagement to be set out in one or more Order Forms incorporating this Agreement by reference;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Documentation" means Halcyon's then-current user guides and specifications for the Services, as updated from time to time.

1.2 "Customer Data" means electronic data submitted by Customer or its Authorized Users to the Services, including patient appointment, messaging and care-plan records processed through Halcyon Engage.

1.3 "Order Form" means an order document referencing this Agreement and specifying the Services subscribed, the Fees and the Subscription Term.

1.4 "Fees" means the amounts payable by Customer under an Order Form.

1.5 "Services" means Halcyon Engage, Halcyon's patient engagement and care coordination software, including appointment reminders, secure messaging, intake forms and care-plan tracking, as further described in the Documentation.

1.6 "Subscription Term" means the Initial Term and each Renewal Term.

1.7 "Affiliate" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities or equivalent voting interest of an entity.

1.8 "Authorized Users" means Customer's employees, contractors and affiliated healthcare providers who are authorized by Customer to access and use the Services under the rights granted to Customer and for whom access has been provisioned in accordance with this Agreement.

1.9 "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure, including the terms of this Agreement, Customer Data, and Halcyon's non-public product, security and roadmap information.

1.10 "Breach" means the acquisition, access, use or disclosure of Customer Data in a manner not permitted by this Agreement that compromises the security, confidentiality or integrity of that data.

1.11 "Scheduled Maintenance" means planned downtime of the Services for upgrades, patches or infrastructure work for which Halcyon gives Customer advance notice in accordance with the Documentation.

1.12 "Malicious Code" means any code, file or program designed to disrupt, damage or gain unauthorized access to a computer system or network, including viruses, worms, time bombs and Trojan horses.

1.13 "Applicable Law" means the laws, regulations and binding regulatory guidance applicable to a Party's performance of its obligations under this Agreement.

2. SERVICES

2.1 Subject to this Agreement and the applicable Order Form, Halcyon shall make the Services available to Customer during the Subscription Term. Halcyon hosts the Services on infrastructure located in AWS `us-east-1` and `us-west-2`, consistent with Halcyon's standard hosting regions for its United States customers.

2.2 Halcyon warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation when used as intended. If the Services do not so perform, Customer's exclusive remedy is for Halcyon, at its own expense, to correct the non-conformity or, if Halcyon cannot do so within a reasonable time, to refund the pro-rated Fees for the affected period.

2.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN SECTION 2.2, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS." HALCYON DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE AND NON-INFRINGEMENT. THE SERVICES ARE NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR PROFESSIONAL MEDICAL JUDGMENT.

2.4 Halcyon will provide Customer with access credentials for a reasonable number of Authorized Users as specified in the applicable Order Form. Customer may add Authorized Users during the Subscription Term, subject to any additional Fees set out in the Order Form or a mutually agreed amendment.

2.5 Halcyon will make available reasonable implementation and onboarding assistance to help Customer configure the Services, including guidance on HL7 FHIR integration with Customer's hospital record systems, as described in the Documentation and any applicable Order Form.

2.6 Customer shall not, and shall ensure that its Authorized Users do not: (a) reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Services; (b) use the Services to transmit Malicious Code; (c) use the Services to store or transmit data in violation of Applicable Law; or (d) use the Services in a way that interferes with Halcyon's provision of the Services to other customers.

2.7 Each Party represents and warrants to the other that: (a) it is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of its jurisdiction of organization; (b) it has full corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations under it; and (c) this Agreement, when executed, constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms.

2.8 Customer further represents and warrants that it has the right to submit the Customer Data to the Services and that doing so, and Halcyon's processing of the Customer Data as contemplated by this Agreement, does not violate the rights of any third party or any Applicable Law.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Customer shall pay the Fees set out in each Order Form. Invoices are issued annually in advance and are due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date ("Net 30").

3.2 Amounts not paid when due accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the highest rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

3.3 Halcyon may increase the Fees upon each renewal by notice at least sixty (60) days before the renewal date, provided the increase does not exceed seven percent (7%) of the prior year's Fees.

3.4 Fees are exclusive of taxes. Customer is responsible for all sales, use, value added and similar taxes other than taxes on Halcyon's net income.

4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 The initial term of each Order Form is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date (the "Initial Term"), unless otherwise stated in the Order Form.

4.2 Following the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews for successive twelve (12)-month terms ("Renewal Terms") unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

4.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for the other Party's material breach if the breach remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach.

4.4 Upon termination or expiration, Customer's right to access the Services ceases, all accrued and unpaid Fees become due, and Halcyon shall make Customer Data available for export for thirty (30) days following the effective date of termination.

5. SUSPENSION

5.1 Halcyon may suspend Customer's access to the Services on reasonable notice for non-payment that remains uncured for ten (10) business days after notice, or immediately and without notice where Halcyon reasonably believes suspension is necessary to prevent harm to the Services or to other customers.

6. SERVICE LEVELS

6.1 Halcyon will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.9%, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and events of force majeure.

6.2 If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below 99.9% in a calendar month, Customer's sole and exclusive remedy is a service credit calculated on a sliding scale, capped at ten percent (10%) of the Fees payable for the affected month.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party shall protect the other Party's Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, but no less than reasonable care, and shall use such information solely for the purpose of performing this Agreement.

7.2 Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was rightfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure by the disclosing Party; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed by the receiving Party without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

7.3 A Party may disclose the other Party's Confidential Information to the extent required by Applicable Law or a valid order of a court or governmental authority, provided that, where legally permitted, it gives the other Party reasonable advance notice so that the other Party may seek a protective order.

8. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

8.1 Halcyon shall use Customer Data solely to provide the Services to Customer, and, in de-identified and aggregated form, to improve the Services and to produce cross-customer benchmarking analytics. Halcyon shall not sell Customer Data or use it for advertising, and shall not use Customer Data to train machine learning models made available to third parties.

8.2 Halcyon maintains a written information security program with administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of Customer Data, consistent with its SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001 and HITRUST r2 certifications. Halcyon will provide a copy of its most recent SOC 2 Type II report upon request within ten (10) business days.

8.3 Halcyon shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of confirming a Breach of Customer Data, describing the nature and scope of the incident to the extent then known.

8.4 Halcyon may engage Sub-processors under its general written authorization. Halcyon will give Customer at least thirty (30) days' notice before engaging a new Sub-processor and Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within fifteen (15) days of notice.

9. AUDIT

9.1 No more than once in any twelve-month period, and on thirty (30) days' prior written notice, Customer may request Halcyon's then-current SOC 2 Type II report and a written security questionnaire response in place of an on-site audit.

9.2 Any report or questionnaire response provided under this Section 9 is Halcyon's Confidential Information, and Customer shall use it solely to assess Halcyon's compliance with this Agreement.

10. COMPLIANCE

10.1 Each Party shall comply with the laws applicable to it in performing this Agreement. Halcyon shall comply with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and equivalent anti-bribery laws applicable to its business.

11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 Except for the Excluded Claims, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the Fees paid and payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the first event giving rise to liability.

11.2 The limitation in Section 11.1 does not apply to: (a) either Party's fraud or wilful misconduct; (b) Customer's payment obligations; and (c) Halcyon's indemnification obligations under Section 13 (together, the "Excluded Claims").

11.3 Notwithstanding Section 11.1, Halcyon's aggregate liability for a Breach of the data protection obligations in Section 8 shall not exceed two (2) times the annual Fees paid by Customer in the preceding twelve months.

11.4 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOSS OF PROFITS, REVENUE, DATA OR GOODWILL, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES. THIS EXCLUSION APPLIES EQUALLY TO BOTH PARTIES AND IS SUBJECT TO THE SAME EXCLUDED CLAIMS LISTED IN SECTION 11.2.
