- **Context**: a dictionary of facts, parties, issues, and instructions relevant to the memo or letter.

- State the purpose clearly in the opening paragraph.
- Analyse the relevant legal or commercial issues concisely.
- Provide clear recommendations or requested actions.

- Do not fabricate facts not present in the context.
